# **Very Basic RPG (VBG) v1.4.2**

> ## **1\. THE BASICS**

This system is designed for asynchronous play. We use d10s (ten-sided dice) and a Pool System. You do not need to wait for the GM to tell you the result of your roll; the dice dictate the narrative outcome immediately.

> ### **The Core Mechanic**

When you attempt a risky action:

1. **Build your Pool:** Add your Stat \+ Relevant Trait to increase you dice pool with said amount.  
2. **Check the Cap:** You may never roll more than **4d10**. If your pool is higher, roll 4 (check Expertise rule).  
3. **Roll:** Roll the dice.  
4. **Check:** Look at the **Single Highest Die**.

> ### **The Expertise Rule (For Pools of 5+)**

The maximum number of dice you can use to determine your result is 4d10 (the Cap).  
However, if your total pool (Stat \+ Trait) is 5 or higher, you have achieved **Expertise**. You still roll only 4 dice to get your result, but you gain one of the following benefits on that roll:

* **Add a Boost:** After you see the result, you can describe a minor, secondary benefit to your action. (e.g., You succeed with a cost, but you also manage to impress a bystander.)  
* **Reduce the Cost:** If you get a "Success with Cost," you can downgrade the severity of the consequence. The GM cannot veto this reduced cost. (e.g., Instead of your sword breaking, it's merely chipped.)

> ### **The Results (Target Number 7\)**

**1 \- 6: FAILURE (The Complication)**  
You do not achieve your goal, or you do so with disastrous consequences.

* *GM Reaction:* The GM will inflict damage, introduce a new threat, or block your path.

**7 \- 9: SUCCESS WITH COST (The Struggle)**  
You achieve your goal, but it’s messy.

* *Player Choice:* You succeed, **BUT** you must pay a cost, mark 1 Strain, break a piece of gear, or alert the enemy. State the cost in your post.  
* *Note:* It is suggested that players opt for a narrative cost Only mark Strain if it makes the most sense or if you can't think of anything else.  
* *Rule against "Softballing" (The GM Veto):* Your cost must match the danger. If you are fighting a dragon and choose "I get mud on my boots" as your cost, the GM will intervene and force a harder consequence (usually damage). Make it hurt.

**10: CRITICAL SUCCESS (The Triumph)**  
You succeed perfectly.

* *Bonus:* Regain 1 Strain OR gain a distinct narrative advantage.

> ## **2\. CHARACTER CREATION**

> ### **A. Stats (The Three F's)**

Start with 0 in all stats. You have **3 Points** to distribute.

* *Start Limit:* No stat may start higher than 2\.  
* *Hard Limit:* No stat may ever exceed 3\.  
* **FORCE:** Raw power, Endurance, intimidation, and blocking/resisting physical harm.  
* **FINESSE:** Agility, stealth, speed, manual dexterity, and precision/aim.  
* **FOCUS:** Intelligence, Senses, mental resilience, knowledge, spotting details.

> ### **B. Traits (Who You Are)**

Traits are narrative truths about your character. They act as your skills.

* **1 MAJOR Trait (+2d10):** Your primary archetype (e.g., Royal Battlemage, Master Thief).  
* **2 MINOR Traits (+1d10):** Hobbies, background, or quirks (e.g., Historian, Bar Brawler, Noble Blood).

> ### **C. Strain (Your Fuel)**

* **Starting Strain:** 5  
* **Max Possible Strain:** 10  
* **Function:** Represents health, mana, and stamina combined.

> ### **D. Ability (Your Power)**

* **One Ability:** This is your unique magical or heroic power source (see [Section 3](#3.-abilities:-the-domain-system)).

> ### **E. Armor**

All characters start with **2 Armor boxes**.

* When you would spend 1 Strain to Soak a hit, you may mark off one of your Armor boxes instead.  
* Armor can only be used to negate incoming harm. It cannot be used to pay for the Strain cost of your own Abilities.  
* Armor is only restored during Downtime (a full rest).

### **F. Inventory (The Slot System)**

**Your capacity to carry gear is limited by Slots.**

* **Base Capacity:** 5 Slots.  
* **Worn vs. Held:** Items worn on the body (Armor, Clothing, Jewelry) do not take up slots. Items held in hands (Weapons, Shields) or carried in packs/pockets do take up slots.

**Item Sizes:**

* **Small:** (Potions, daggers, scrolls, keys) — Stack up to 4 in 1 Slot.  
* **Medium:** (One-handed weapons, tools, rope, rations) — Takes 1 Slot.  
* **Large:** (Two-handed weapons, heavy equipment) — Takes 2 Slots.

**Containers:**

* Backpack: Equipping a backpack grants \+5 Slots (Total Capacity: 10).


> ## **3\. ABILITIES: The Domain System** {#3.-abilities:-the-domain-system}

You define a Domain for your character.

> ### **Defining Your Ability**

Name your Ability and write one sentence defining what it controls.

* *Example:* Pyromancy: The control and generation of heat and flame.  
* Using Abilities does not require a specific stat; you choose the stat based on how you use the power.  
* Can be created solely and Verified by the GM or By collaborating with the GM.

> ### **Using Abilities (The Scale)**

You can attempt anything within your Domain using Focus (for control), Force (for power), or Finesse (for aiming). The cost is determined by the Scale of the effect:

**MINOR (Cost: 0 Strain)**

* *Effect:* Cosmetic effects, sensory changes, lighting candles, minor attacks.  
* *Mechanic:* Just Roll.

**STANDARD (Cost: 1 Strain)**

* *Effect:* Combat attacks, overcoming/creating obstacles, affecting two targets, mobility.  
* *Mechanic:* Spend 1 Strain \+ Roll.

**MAJOR (Cost: 2 Strain)**

* *Effect:* Multi-target attacks (small groups), incapacitating a strong foe, significant battlefield alteration (great walls, zones of control), elite feats of athleticism.  
* *Mechanic:* Spend 2 Strain \+ Roll.

**MIRACLE (Cost: 3 Strain)**

* *Effect:* Room-clearing attacks, feats of god-like strength, permanent environmental change, defying the laws of physics entirely.  
* *Mechanic:* Spend 3 Strain \+ Roll.

> ## **4\. COMBAT & SURVIVAL**

> ### **Taking Damage**

There are no HP. When an enemy hits you or you fail a dangerous roll:

1. **Take the Hit:** The GM describes a narrative wound (broken arm, concussion). This may apply a temporary negative Trait.  
2. **Soak It (Spend 1 Strain/Armor):** You exert yourself to dodge, parry, or power through the pain. The narrative wound is ignored or reduced to a scratch.

> ### **Using Armor**

All characters start with 2 Armor boxes.

* When you would spend 1 Strain to Soak a hit, you may mark off one of your Armor boxes instead.  
* Armor can only be used to negate incoming harm. It cannot be used to pay for the Strain cost of your own Abilities.  
* Armor is only restored during Downtime (a full rest).

> ### **Being Taken Out**

If you have 0 Strain and take a hit, you are **Taken Out**. You are unconscious, captured, or dying (GM discretion).

> ### **Recovery (Resting)**

* **Catch Your Breath:** Once per scene, recover 3 Strain.  
* **Downtime (Full Rest):** Recovers All Strain and restores All Armor.  
* **Critical:** Rolling a 10 recovers 1 Strain.

> ## **5\. PROGRESSION ECONOMY**

The GM awards **PP (Progression Points)** for completing missions, good roleplay, and posting consistency (i.e. resolving threads). All upgrades are purchased directly with PP.

* **\[10 PP\] Increase Strain Cap:** Increase Max Strain by \+1 (Max Cap: 10).  
* **\[15 PP\] New Ability Domain:** Unlock a new magical/skill domain.  
* **\[20 PP\] New Minor Trait:** Add a new \+1d10 background trait.  
* **\[20 PP\] Upgrade Trait:** Turn a Minor Trait (+1d10) into a Major Trait (+2d10).  
* **\[25 PP\] Stat Increase:** \+1 to Force, Finesse, or Focus (Max Stat: 3).
