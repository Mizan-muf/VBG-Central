# Echoes

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses one or two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Any continuing Energy cost uses a recorded **Energy/min** rate; numerical rates, storage costs and capacity, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Aspect:** Sounds personally captured by the user.
- **Expressions:** Capture stores a sound reaching a cupped hand; Release plays it from that hand in a chosen direction. Capture does not silence the sound for other listeners.
- **Anchor:** Cup a hand to capture, then close it to retain the echo. Open it deliberately to release; forcing it open disperses the stored sound. Directed delivery reaches up to Close / Room, subject to acoustics and obstructions.
- **Mark:** Concentric rings in the palms contract around a held echo and spread during release.
- **Boundary:** Cannot invent words, amplify captured sound, reproduce supernatural powers, or place a sound source remotely. A captured passage is spent when released; storage limits and retention remain open.

## Borrowed Command

Keywords: Capture + Release : Veil : Burst : Cone : Person-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Catch a short spoken phrase and immediately play it towards a listener from a concealed hand. The cone is person-wide at its far end.

**Output:** Reproduce the captured voice and words, potentially misleading someone about who spoke nearby.

**Mastery benefit:** Preserve clear diction and cadence through the directional release.

**Requirement / interruption:** A suitable audible phrase and a concealed outlet if deception requires it. Listeners can recognise the trick; the phrase is not a command they must obey.

**Aftermath:** The echo is spent and travels as ordinary sound. Interrupting the hand before release spoils delivery.

## Corner Knock

Keywords: Release : Veil : Burst : Cone : Palm-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Direct a previously captured knock towards a nearby hard surface so its reflection draws attention there. The narrow cone is palm-wide at that surface.

**Output:** Produce an audible distraction whose apparent direction depends on the room's acoustics.

**Mastery benefit:** Aim the reflection at a chosen visible surface with greater directional precision.

**Requirement / interruption:** A stored knock, an open delivery path, and a useful reflecting surface. The base cost covers release; obtaining and retaining the echo is separate.

**Aftermath:** The echo is spent. The sound originates at the hand and reflects naturally; it does not teleport behind cover.
