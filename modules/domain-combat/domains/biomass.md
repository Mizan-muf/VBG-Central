# Biomass

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Generated biomass persists only while its Sustain upkeep is paid at the technique's recorded **Energy/min** rate. Numerical upkeep rates, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Name:** Biomass
- **Origin:** Internal Energy
- **Aspect:**
  - **Keyword:** `Biomass`
  - **Explanation:** Inert, nonliving organic biological matter generated from Internal Energy.
- **Expressions:**
  - **Keywords:** `Generate` + `Shape`
  - **Generate:** *(Operation Keyword)* Creates inert biomass from Internal Energy.
  - **Shape:** *(Operation Keyword)* Arranges biomass generated by this Domain while it remains connected to the palm.
- **Anchor:**
  - **Keywords:** `Open Palm` (Medium) · `Melee / Reach` (Range Band)
  - **Establish:** An open palm establishes generation and shaping out to Melee / Reach.
  - **Reach:** Up to Melee / Reach while maintaining physical connection.
  - **Maintain:** Keep an unbroken physical connection to the palm and pay the technique's recorded Sustain upkeep.
  - **Break:** A severed physical connection ends active control over that portion; ending upkeep causes the biomass to dissolve.
- **Mark:** Branching lines across the palms extend towards the wrists when biomass is generated or shaped.
- **Boundary:** Generated biomass is nonliving. It grants no healing, Energy recovery, copied abilities, senses, control of other bodies, or immunity to harmful material. It persists only while its recorded Sustain **Energy/min** upkeep is paid.

## Borrowed Grip

- **Keywords:** `Generate + Shape` (Expressions) : `Bind` (Form) : `Sustain` (Flow) : `Custom (tendril)` (Shape) : `Custom (arm-thick)` (Size) : `Melee / Reach` (Range)

**Method:** Generate and shape fibrous biomass from an open palm into a tendril that wraps around a reachable limb or object.

**Output:** Restrain the caught part against the tendril's agreed strength.

**Mastery benefit:** Maintain a precise grip on a small handle or moving joint without crushing it.

**Requirement / interruption:** An open palm establishes the tendril and its recorded **Energy/min** upkeep sustains it. Losing its connection, severing it, or overcoming its grip defeats the active restraint.

**Aftermath:** The tendril dissolves when upkeep ends. A disconnected but sustained tendril remains physical but cannot continue tightening afterward.

## Grafted Buckler

- **Keywords:** `Generate + Shape` (Expressions) : `Ward` (Form) : `Sustain` (Flow) : `Custom (buckler)` (Shape) : `Custom (forearm-sized)` (Size) : `Self / Touch` (Range)

**Method:** Generate and shape layered biomass from an open palm into a shield attached to the forearm.

**Output:** Create a physical guard whose protection follows its recorded material properties.

**Mastery benefit:** Shape a fitted wrist brace that helps keep the shield aligned during an impact it can withstand.

**Requirement / interruption:** An open palm establishes the guard and its recorded **Energy/min** upkeep sustains it. Disrupting its form or stopping upkeep ends its protection.

**Aftermath:** The buckler dissolves when upkeep ends. Damage stays damage; its formation does not restore Strain.
