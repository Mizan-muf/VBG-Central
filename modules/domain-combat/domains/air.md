# Air

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses one or two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Any continuing Energy cost uses a recorded **Energy/min** rate; numerical rates, storage costs and capacity, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Aspect:** Existing air currents and pressure differences.
- **Expressions:** Compress packs air into a smaller space; Redirect changes the direction of an existing current, including one produced by releasing compressed air.
- **Anchor:** Breath establishes influence beside the mouth. Maintain an uninterrupted breath and connected airflow to control it out to Close / Room. Breaking either ends control; released air continues naturally.
- **Mark:** Curved lines around the lips and throat ripple outward during controlled breathing.
- **Boundary:** Cannot generate air, affect air inside a living body, or control weather. Pressure and recoil can harm the user.

## Air Pistol

Keywords: Compress + Redirect : Project : Burst : Bolt : Palm-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Compress air at the mouth and redirect its release into a narrow shot along a clear path.

**Output:** Strike one exposed target with a blunt pressure impact.

**Mastery benefit:** The impact also pushes a loose, lightly held object from the struck point when the delivered force can dislodge it; it does not guarantee a disarm.

**Requirement / interruption:** A usable breath and clear outlet. Disrupting the breath before release spoils the shot.

**Aftermath:** The shot disperses and cannot be steered after release.

## Crosswind Guard

Keywords: Redirect : Ward : Sustain : Wall : Person-sized : Melee / Reach

**Method:** Turn an existing current into a crosswind across the front of the body.

**Output:** Deflect loose dust, smoke, and light airborne debris that the current can move.

**Mastery benefit:** Leave a controlled viewing slit through the moving screen.

**Requirement / interruption:** Enough existing airflow for the threat; a still room does not supply a strong defensive wind. Broken breath or disconnected airflow ends the screen.

**Aftermath:** Displaced matter remains wherever ordinary airflow carries it. This is not a solid shield.
