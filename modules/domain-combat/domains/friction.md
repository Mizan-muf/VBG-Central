# Friction

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses one or two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Any continuing Energy cost uses a recorded **Energy/min** rate; numerical rates, storage costs and capacity, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Aspect:** Friction between nonliving contacting surfaces.
- **Expressions:** Increase raises resistance to sliding; Reduce lowers it.
- **Anchor:** Establish influence through bodily contact with one surface at the affected interface, including worn footwear or gloves. Only the local contact patch is affected. Losing that connection ends the change immediately.
- **Mark:** Ridges across the palms and soles sharpen or smooth visually as friction changes.
- **Boundary:** Cannot propel an object without force, create adhesion across a gap, strengthen materials, or protect against falls and sudden stops. Ordinary friction returns when control ends.

## Lockstep

Keywords: Reduce + Increase : Shift : Sustain : Coating : Custom (footwear contact) : Self / Touch

**Method:** Lower friction beneath the footwear while pushing off, then raise it progressively to brake at a declared destination within Close.

**Output:** Slide along a continuous clear surface using the user's momentum. Close is the movement limit, not remote control range.

**Mastery benefit:** Finish in a controlled stance when the available braking distance and surface can safely absorb the momentum.

**Requirement / interruption:** Ground contact, a usable route, and an initial push. Leaving the surface or losing footing interrupts controlled travel; the technique cannot cancel excessive momentum safely.

**Aftermath:** Ordinary friction resumes. A jump does not carry the effect through the air.

## Surehold

Keywords: Increase : Ward : Sustain : Coating : Palm-sized : Self / Touch

**Method:** Increase friction between worn gloves and a held nonliving object.

**Output:** Resist the object slipping or being pulled through the grip.

**Mastery benefit:** Keep the friction change confined to the intended gripping patches, leaving nearby contacts free to move.

**Requirement / interruption:** Gloves, sustained contact, and a grip the body can support. Losing contact ends the effect; excessive force can still move or injure the user.

**Aftermath:** No adhesion remains after release.
