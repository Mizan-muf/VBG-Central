# Knots

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses one or two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Any continuing Energy cost uses a recorded **Energy/min** rate; numerical rates, storage costs and capacity, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Aspect:** Knots personally tied in flexible material.
- **Expressions:** Tighten cinches an existing knot; Loosen relaxes it, including fully untying it.
- **Anchor:** Tie the knot through touch. Control remains possible within Close / Room while the knot is intact; it grants no remote perception. Cutting or fully untying it ends the connection.
- **Mark:** Interlaced lines on the fingers draw taut during tightening and relax during loosening.
- **Boundary:** Cannot create rope, tie new knots remotely, move an entire cord independently, or affect someone else's knots. Adjusted knots remain physically tied after control ends.

## Snarestep

Keywords: Tighten : Bind : Burst : Custom (loop) : Custom (limb-sized) : Close / Room

**Method:** Physically cast a prepared knotted loop around a limb or object, then cinch it.

**Output:** Catch and hinder what the loop successfully encloses, within the cord's strength.

**Mastery benefit:** Use a practised trigger to cinch at the visible moment of contact, reducing premature closure.

**Requirement / interruption:** A throwable cord and a visible target. A miss, cut cord, or broken knot prevents restraint; the cord does not guide itself.

**Aftermath:** The knot remains tied until loosened, cut, or physically overcome.

## Slipknot Exit

Keywords: Loosen : Shift : Burst : Custom (knot) : Palm-sized : Self / Touch

**Method:** Loosen a personally tied fastening securing the user's cloak, harness, or pack.

**Output:** Free the user from that fastening so the caught equipment can be shed.

**Mastery benefit:** Stop at a prepared loose configuration that can be physically fastened again without rethreading the whole assembly.

**Requirement / interruption:** The fastening must be the user's own intact knot. Other buckles, restraints, or an enemy holding the body still need separate answers.

**Aftermath:** Released equipment stays where it falls. Fully untying the knot ends its Anchor.
