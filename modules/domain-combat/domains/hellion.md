This draft set offers two Mastered techniques for the character's two free starting choices. Numerical outputs and upkeep remain unresolved under Domain Combat; approve them with the GM before play.

A second spine rises beneath the skin. Extra ribs crawl around the chest, muscle cords wind around the limbs, and pale plates close over the face like a second jaw. When the user moves, the armor parts along wet seams and the anatomy underneath pulls them forward.

Hellion turns the body into a hulking siege creature. Its visual identity is an ivory cage over bruised violet muscle, with tooth-like plate edges and a raised vertebral ridge. The transformation feels like something inside the user unfolding into its adult shape.

## Domain

- **Name:** Hellion.
- **Origin:** Internal Energy.
- **Aspect:** A temporary war anatomy generated from the user's back: a supporting vertebral frame, contractile muscle cords, and rigid interlocking plates, all physically joined to the user.
- **Expressions:**
  - **Generate:** Grow this anatomy from Internal Energy at the back and along its connected frame. Its plates are naturally hard and its muscle cords can contract; generation supplies additional mass without consuming the user's original tissue.
  - **Articulate:** Arrange and contract the generated anatomy around the user's body: seat its plates, position its supports, and drive its muscles in concert with the user's movement. This does not change its material properties.
- **Anchor:**
  - **Establish:** Send a deliberate pulse of Internal Energy through the mark along the spine; growth begins beneath the skin of the back.
  - **Reach:** Self. The anatomy must remain attached to the back and fitted around the user's body.
  - **Maintain:** Remain conscious and keep the generated anatomy physically connected to its root at the back. No touch, exposed skin, fixed posture, or continuous back tension is required.
  - **Break:** Voluntary release, unconsciousness, or destruction of the generated root ends the connection. Severing a section disconnects only that section. An ordinary blow to the back does not automatically break the Anchor.
- **Boundary:** Creates one attached bodily frame, within the size and output recorded for the technique. It cannot become a separate creature, extend independent limbs or tendrils, consume bodies, copy traits, or heal the user's injuries. Rigid clothing or equipment can obstruct growth; equipment does not transform with the user.
- **Tolerance:** The user can endure the anatomy's emergence, attachment, and ordinary contraction without losing Strain merely for transforming. Its frame supports its own weight and routes its approved muscular force through reinforced joints. Protection comes from actual coverage and material strength; injury reaching the original body and exertion beyond the recorded output can still reduce Strain. Heat, suffocation, and poison remain threats.
- **Persistence:** Attached anatomy requires its technique's Sustain upkeep. When upkeep or the Anchor ends, it folds toward the back and crumbles into inert flakes that fade without leaving usable material. Disconnected sections immediately become inert and decay the same way. Injuries to the original body remain; reforming damaged armor requires a new paid application of Generate.
- **Mark:** A row of pale, tooth-shaped ridges under the skin follows the spine. On activation they lift in sequence from the lower back to the neck, and dark branching lines spread across the shoulder blades. The mark itself grants no extra weak point.

## Dreadnought Flesh

- **Type / status:** Suggested starting Mastered technique; draft pending GM approval.
- **Access:** Hellion.
- **Keywords:** Generate + Articulate : Shape : Sustain : Coating : Person-sized : Self / Touch
- **Method:** Unfold a second vertebral frame from the back and wrap it around the body. Muscle cords sleeve the arms and legs; overlapping plates enclose the torso, shoulders, limbs, and head. A split face guard leaves openings for ordinary sight and breathing.
- **Dimensions and delivery:** Self only. A close-fitting full-body transformation with a broadened torso and shoulders; the user's existing arms and legs remain its only limbs. It must fit the available space.
- **Anchor use:** The internal spinal pulse starts growth. The back root remains connected throughout the transformation; the hands stay free.
- **Base output:** Physical armor and enhanced bodily strength through the frame's supporting structure and added muscles. It improves lifting, pushing, grappling, and melee force within an agreed output; protection and strength values are **Unresolved**. It grants no extra actions or automatic success.
- **Mastery benefit:** A fitted collar lets the user turn the head and look behind a shoulder while retaining overlapping neck coverage.
- **Conditions:** Plates lock together when the body braces, limiting flexibility. Powerful movement parts the overlaps at the working joints, exposing muscle cords there. Grips remain coarse; delicate handling requires opening the relevant hand plates.
- **Requirements:** Space for the enlarged silhouette and clothing or equipment that accommodates growth. A trapped limb cannot transform through a rigid obstruction.
- **Action demand:** Major to transform or substantially rearrange the frame; Minor for steady maintenance each exchange. Attacks and committed defences still use the normal Major Action.
- **Expression applications:** Two: Generate creates the frame; Articulate fits and operates it as one sustained transformation.
- **Base Energy:** 2 improvised / 1 Mastered.
- **Other Energy costs:** Sustain rate **Unresolved Energy/min**. Agree on the rate and output before play; the unresolved rate is not free.
- **Interruption:** Losing the Anchor or upkeep ends the form. Broken plates expose the affected area; severed muscle cords reduce the support or force they supplied until rebuilt.
- **Aftermath:** The anatomy withdraws and decays. Damage, displaced objects, and consequences of the user's actions remain.
- **Acquisition:** May fill one of the two free starting Mastered choices; otherwise requires GM approval and a PP price, currently **Unresolved**.

## Ruin Drive

- **Type / status:** Suggested starting Mastered technique; draft pending GM approval.
- **Access:** Hellion, with Dreadnought Flesh or an equivalent approved sustained frame already active.
- **Keywords:** Articulate : Project : Burst : Custom (bodily impact) : Custom (one shoulder or fist) : Melee / Reach
- **Method:** Draw the frame's muscle cords taut from the planted foot through the back, then contract them together into one shoulder ram or straight punch. Plates slide apart along the working joints as the reinforced body drives through the strike.
- **Dimensions and delivery:** One target already within Melee / Reach, struck by physical contact. Any approach uses ordinary movement along a clear route; the technique grants no ranged shockwave or additional movement allowance.
- **Anchor use:** Operates the existing frame through its connected root at the back.
- **Base output:** One concentrated blunt impact using the frame's enhanced strength. Its damage is **Unresolved** and must be recorded relative to an ordinary strike in Dreadnought Flesh.
- **Mastery benefit:** On a successful hit with enough force to move the target, carry it one step directly backward into available space. This does not guarantee a knockdown or movement through an obstacle.
- **Conditions:** Requires a planted foot and a clear striking path. The committed shoulder or arm leaves its joint seams exposed until the action resolves; a miss can leave the user overextended.
- **Requirements:** An intact supporting route through the frame's leg, back, and striking limb. The surface beneath the user must bear the push.
- **Action demand:** One Major Action. It answers an incoming threat only if the declared strike and positioning actually do so.
- **Expression applications:** One: Articulate contracts the existing muscle cords into one coordinated impact.
- **Base Energy:** 1 improvised / 1 Mastered, in addition to the existing frame's upkeep.
- **Other Energy costs:** No separate Sustain effect; the frame's recorded **Energy/min** upkeep continues.
- **Interruption:** Losing footing, the Anchor, or the supporting muscle connection spoils delivery. A missed or interrupted attempt does not refund Energy.
- **Aftermath:** The sustained frame remains in its current condition. The target and surroundings retain ordinary impact consequences; nothing stays charged.
- **Acquisition:** May fill the other free starting Mastered choice; otherwise requires GM approval and a PP price, currently **Unresolved**.
