# Lepricon

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses one or two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Any continuing Energy cost uses a recorded **Energy/min** rate; numerical rates, storage costs and capacity, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Aspect:** Solid metallic coins, ranging from small tokens to large bullion discs, generated from Internal Energy at the user's palms or fingertips.
- **Expressions:** Generate creates solid metallic coins from Internal Energy at the user's palms or fingertips; Propel shoots coins generated by this Domain outward along a clear visible path with sudden velocity.
- **Anchor:** An open palm or pinching fingertips establish generation. Active control (propulsion) requires line of sight and an unobstructed delivery path within Close / Room; closing the hand, losing line of sight, or releasing the shot ends active control. Released coins continue along their trajectory under ordinary momentum.
- **Mark:** Embossed circular ridges resembling coin rims ring the knuckles and fingertips, gleaming like burnished brass or minted gold when coins are produced or launched.
- **Boundary:** Cannot create genuine currency recognized by mints or legal banks, alter existing currency, change metal composition, create precious gems, manipulate chance or wealth, track coins remotely, or guide coins around cover after release. Coins unanchored or released travel on ordinary physical momentum and fall inert. Generated coins persist only while their recorded Sustain upkeep is paid, or dissolve into faint metallic dust after impact or release. Recoil and rapid discharge can strain the fingers or hand.

## Bullion Shot

Keywords: Generate + Propel : Project : Burst : Bolt : Palm-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Generate a solid, dense metallic coin between the thumb and forefinger and propel it outward along a clear visible path with sudden velocity.

**Output:** Strike one exposed target with a concentrated blunt metal impact.

**Mastery benefit:** Impart a tight stabilizing spin to keep the coin traveling edge-first, reducing flutter and maintaining accuracy against light crosswinds.

**Requirement / interruption:** An open launching hand, line of sight, and a clear delivery path. Solid cover, an obstructed flight path, or spoiling the launch grip ruins delivery; the coin cannot track or steer around obstructions.

**Aftermath:** The coin continues under ordinary momentum, strikes or lands as an inert metal token, and dissolves into faint dust after use.

## Toll Aegis

Keywords: Generate : Ward : Sustain : Custom (coin disc) : Person-sized : Self / Touch

**Method:** Generate a thick, large circular coin disc from an open palm or braced forearm, holding it by its raised rim or stamped relief.

**Output:** Interpose a solid metallic barrier to block or deflect physical strikes, ranged projectiles, and debris within the coin's physical diameter and material strength.

**Mastery benefit:** Shape a narrow sight notch along the upper coin rim, allowing the user to observe incoming threats without peering around the edge.

**Requirement / interruption:** An open palm or braced forearm and the technique's recorded **Energy/min** upkeep. Disproportionate force can buckle the metal or transfer concussive shock to the bracing arm; breaking contact or ending upkeep causes the disc to collapse.

**Aftermath:** The coin disc dissolves into faint metallic dust when upkeep ends. Damage that bypassed or overwhelmed the barrier remains as ordinary injury.
