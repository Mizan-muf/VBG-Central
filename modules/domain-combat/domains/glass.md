# Glass

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses one or two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Any continuing Energy cost uses a recorded **Energy/min** rate; numerical rates, storage costs and capacity, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Aspect:** Broken, nonliving glass personally touched by the user.
- **Expressions:** Gather draws eligible pieces together through visible space; Shape arranges and holds rigid fragments in a chosen structure without fusing them.
- **Anchor:** Touch each piece to establish eligibility. Active control requires sight within Close / Room; losing either sight or range releases that piece. Touch alone does not provide remote sensing.
- **Mark:** Faceted lines cross the fingertips and catch the light when pieces are gathered.
- **Boundary:** Cannot break intact glass, generate it, soften it, or grant cut resistance. Unsupported arrangements fall when control ends; pieces remain ordinary glass.

## Shard Canopy

Keywords: Gather + Shape : Ward : Sustain : Wall : Person-sized : Melee / Reach

**Method:** Gather touched shards into a tilted screen in front of the user.

**Output:** Interpose brittle material against threats it can physically block or deflect.

**Mastery benefit:** Maintain a narrow viewing gap while repositioning the surrounding fragments.

**Requirement / interruption:** Enough eligible glass and an unobstructed gathering route. Broken sight releases affected shards; a strong impact may shatter or scatter them.

**Aftermath:** Released shards fall and remain a hazard.

## Mirror Mosaic

Keywords: Gather + Shape : Shape : Sustain : Custom (reflective panel) : Palm-sized : Self / Touch

**Method:** Assemble touched, reflective fragments into a small panel above the palm.

**Output:** Provide an ordinary reflected view from the panel's chosen angle.

**Mastery benefit:** Align the fragments into a steadier, readable image instead of separate disjointed reflections.

**Requirement / interruption:** Suitable reflective fragments and direct sight of the assembly. The view still depends on lighting and the reflection's physical path.

**Aftermath:** The assembly separates when released. It grants no vision through opaque objects.
