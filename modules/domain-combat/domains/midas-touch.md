# Midas Touch

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Any continuing Energy cost uses a recorded **Energy/min** rate; numerical rates, storage costs and capacity, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Name:** Midas Touch
- **Origin:** Internal Energy
- **Aspect:**
  - **Keyword:** `Liquid Gold`
  - **Explanation:** Viscous liquid gold generated from Internal Energy through the user's bare skin, capable of fluid secretion, pressurized spray, and rapid phase transition into hardened, solid gold.
- **Expressions:**
  - **Keywords:** `Generate` + `Harden`
  - **Generate:** *(Operation Keyword)* Creates heavy, luminous liquid gold from Internal Energy, secreting it through the user's exposed skin (pores, palms, fingertips, or forearms); it can flow freely, pool, coat surfaces, or be discharged under bodily pressure as a pressurized spray.
  - **Harden:** *(Operation Keyword)* Solidifies liquid gold produced by this Domain into dense, rigid metal at will, locking its fluid form into unyielding solid gold and increasing its structural resistance.
- **Anchor:**
  - **Keywords:** `Bare Skin Contact` (Medium) · `Self / Touch` to `Close / Room` (Range Bands)
  - **Establish:** Bare skin contact establishes secretion at the palms, fingertips, or forearms.
  - **Reach:** Physical contact or an unbroken fluid stream out to Close / Room; disconnected airborne spray or splatters can be hardened if kept within unobstructed line of sight and Close / Room before dispersing.
  - **Maintain:** Keep the secreting skin uncovered and active, or maintain line of sight and the technique's recorded Sustain upkeep for existing coats and structures.
  - **Break:** Covering or binding the secreting skin, losing line of sight before hardening, voluntary cessation, unconsciousness, or ending upkeep breaks active control.
- **Mark:** Intricate filigree veins resembling liquid gold capillary networks spread beneath and across the skin of the palms, wrists, and forearms. When the Anchor engages, these filigree lines pulse with a molten amber-gold luster, and beads of liquid gold shimmer across the pores of the hands.
- **Boundary:** Cannot transmute existing external objects, materials, or living creatures into gold (this is generation from Internal Energy, not mythological transmutation). Cannot create legal currency, certified bullion, or minted coins recognized by banks or mints. Cannot manipulate natural or mined gold not produced by this Domain. Grants no inherent immunity to heat, concussive force, or the physical encumbrance and dead weight of heavy solid gold. Disconnected liquid gold unhardened by the user flows and falls under ordinary gravity and momentum; it cannot be steered remotely in mid-air. Generated gold persists only while its recorded Sustain upkeep is paid at the technique's Energy/min rate, or dissolves into faint, inert metallic dust after impact or release.
- **Tolerance:** Ordinary bodily tolerance. The user's epidermal layers are adapted to secrete and endure the passage of dense liquid gold without thermal blistering or tearing, but rapid, high-volume secretion causes dermal fatigue and dehydration. Carrying or wearing hardened solid gold places normal physical strain on the user's muscles and joints.
- **Persistence:** Generated gold (both liquid and hardened) persists only while its recorded Sustain upkeep is paid at the technique's Energy/min rate. When upkeep ends or active control is broken, the gold dissolves into faint, inert metallic dust or brittle flakes. Damage or physical obstruction caused during its presence remains.

## Auric Torrent

- **Keywords:** `Generate + Harden` (Expressions) : `Project` (Form) : `Burst` (Flow) : `Stream` (Shape) : `Palm-sized` (Size) : `Close / Room` (Range)

**Method:** Secrete a pressurized stream of viscous liquid gold from open palms or forearms, directing it along a clear visible path across Close / Room, and flash-harden the liquid as it strikes an exposed target or surface.

**Output:** Strike one exposed target with a concentrated heavy metallic impact that inflicts blunt damage, while splattering and hardening dense gold over their limbs, gear, or footing to hinder movement or weight down the struck section.

**Mastery benefit:** Aim specifically at a held weapon, shield boss, trigger mechanism, or armor articulation; the hardened gold encases the mechanism or hinge, seizing it rigid until physically pried, broken, or scraped off.

**Requirement / interruption:** An open launching hand or exposed forearm, line of sight, and a clear delivery path. Solid cover, an obstructed flight path, or spoiling the launch stance ruins delivery; the stream cannot steer around solid cover. Breaking line of sight before impact causes the liquid gold to splatter inertly without hardening.

**Aftermath:** The stream continues under ordinary momentum, strikes as a heavy fluid mass, and flash-hardens on impact. Hardened gold dissolves into faint metallic dust when its upkeep or effect duration ends. Struck targets retain ordinary impact bruising and physical encumbrance while the crust remains.

## Gilded Aegis

- **Keywords:** `Generate + Harden` (Expressions) : `Ward` (Form) : `Sustain` (Flow) : `Coating` (Shape) : `Person-sized` (Size) : `Self / Touch` (Range)

**Method:** Exude a layer of viscous liquid gold across the skin of an exposed forearm, limb, or torso, immediately hardening it into a dense, contoured plate of solid gold fitted to the body.

**Output:** Interpose a dense metallic barrier to absorb, deflect, or block physical strikes, cutting edges, and ranged projectiles within the gold's physical coverage and agreed material thickness.

**Mastery benefit:** Shape an angled deflection ridge along the outer rim of the hardened plate, helping redirect kinetic force and glancing blows away from the bracing arm rather than absorbing the full concussive shock directly into the bone.

**Requirement / interruption:** Exposed bare skin on the bracing limb or torso and the technique's recorded **Energy/min** upkeep. Disproportionate force can buckle the plate or transmit concussive shock into the underlying flesh; losing skin contact, unconsciousness, or ending upkeep causes the gold to disintegrate.

**Aftermath:** The golden plate dissolves into fine, inert metallic dust when upkeep ends. Damage that penetrated or bypassed the guard remains as ordinary injury.
