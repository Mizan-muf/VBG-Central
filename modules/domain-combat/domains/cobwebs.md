# Cobwebs

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Generated filament persists only while its Sustain upkeep is paid at the technique's recorded **Energy/min** rate. Numerical upkeep rates, tensile strength, adhesive strength, and control remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Aspect:** Adhesive cobweb filament generated from the user's fingertips.
- **Expressions:** Generate produces adhesive filament at an exposed fingertip; Weave joins and arranges filament generated by this Domain into a connected web.
- **Anchor:** An exposed fingertip begins a strand. Keep that fingertip in contact with the strand's root and an unbroken filament path to control its connected web out to Close / Room. Covering or separating the fingertip, cutting the only connecting path, or ending upkeep ends active control.
- **Mark:** Fine silver thread-lines spiral across the fingertips and draw taut toward the wrist while the Anchor holds.
- **Boundary:** Cannot generate spiders, venom, living material, or silk from another location; move a web independently after it is set; sense through a strand; or make a web stronger than its agreed physical material. Webs entangle only through their physical contact and grant no immunity to adhesive or cutting harm. Generated filament shrivels when its Sustain upkeep ends.

## Snare Lattice

Keywords: Generate + Weave : Bind : Sustain : Custom (anchored lattice) : Person-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Draw connected filaments from a rooted fingertip between visible solid supports, weaving a person-sized lattice across a declared route or around an exposed limb.

**Output:** Hinder a creature or object that becomes physically caught in the lattice, within the web's agreed adhesive and tensile strength.

**Mastery benefit:** Leave one declared break-away seam so force that parts it opens only that section instead of unravelling the entire lattice.

**Requirement / interruption:** A rooted exposed fingertip, a clear filament route, suitable visible supports or an exposed target part, and the technique's recorded **Energy/min** upkeep. Cutting the web, pulling free with sufficient force, blocking its placement, or severing its only connecting path defeats the restraint.

**Aftermath:** The lattice remains a physical obstruction while upkeep continues. It shrivels when upkeep ends; anything already freed stays free.

## Gossamer Screen

Keywords: Generate + Weave : Veil : Sustain : Wall : Person-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Pin a connected, layered web between visible solid edges and weave it into a hanging screen across a doorway, window, or similarly sized opening.

**Output:** Obscure sight through the covered opening where the web is dense enough, while physically catching only objects its material can stop.

**Mastery benefit:** Leave a palm-wide viewing slit from one declared side without opening a person-sized route through the screen.

**Requirement / interruption:** A rooted exposed fingertip, visible attachment edges, a clear route for the filaments, and the technique's recorded **Energy/min** upkeep. Cutting, burning, tearing, or bypassing the screen defeats the relevant covered section; losing the rooted connection prevents adjustment.

**Aftermath:** The screen shrivels when upkeep ends. Debris caught in it falls or continues normally; the web does not conceal what remains visible around it.
