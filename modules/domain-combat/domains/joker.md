# Joker

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses one Expression application, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Numerical damage and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Aspect:** Intact playing cards personally marked by the user with a hand-drawn joker.
- **Expressions:** Flick launches one marked card from an open hand along a chosen clear path; Recall pulls one visible marked card through unobstructed air to an open hand.
- **Anchor:** Draw the joker by hand on the card to establish the connection. Control reaches the marked card within Close / Room while the user can see it and keeps the receiving hand open. Erasing the mark, tearing the card, losing sight or range, or closing the receiving hand ends active control.
- **Mark:** A red-and-black diamond sits in the centre of each palm. Its corners stretch into tiny bells while a card leaves or returns to that hand.
- **Boundary:** Cannot create, alter, sharpen, or reinforce cards; affect another person's unmarked cards; see through a card; guide a card around cover; pull an object held or secured by another creature; or change chance, a draw, or any outcome. A launched or recalled card travels as ordinary paper once active control ends.

## Fool's Cut

Keywords: Flick : Project : Burst : Card : Palm-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Snap one marked card from an open hand, launching it edge-first along a clear visible path at one exposed target or object.

**Output:** Deliver one card-sized physical strike. Its damage or cutting effect remains limited by the card's actual material and the technique's agreed output; clothing, armour, and hard surfaces can stop it.

**Mastery benefit:** Set the card to arrive face-up, face-down, or edge-first at the declared point without losing its line of travel.

**Requirement / interruption:** An intact marked card, an open launching hand, and a clear path. Cover, a spoiled throw, or destroying the card defeats the delivery.

**Aftermath:** The card lands or continues under ordinary momentum. It remains a usable marked card if intact, but has no further guided movement until recalled or launched again.

## Jester's Fetch

Keywords: Recall : Shift : Burst : Card : Palm-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Hold an open marked palm toward one visible marked card and draw it back along a clear route.

**Output:** Retrieve the card to that hand, or make it cross the stated route as a brief, visible distraction. The card can move only through space it could physically pass.

**Mastery benefit:** Catch the returning card between two chosen fingers, leaving the rest of the hand available for an ordinary grip.

**Requirement / interruption:** The card must be intact, visible, unheld, and within range; its route must be unobstructed. A creature can stop it with ordinary cover, a closed grip, or a sufficiently firm hold.

**Aftermath:** The card rests in the receiving hand and is no longer moving. It does not bring other objects, reveal what it passed, or pull the user toward it.
