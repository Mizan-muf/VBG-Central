# Electricity

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses one or two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Any continuing Energy cost uses a recorded **Energy/min** rate; numerical rates, storage costs and capacity, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Aspect:** Electrical discharges generated at the user's fingertips.
- **Expressions:** Generate produces a discharge at an exposed fingertip; Redirect chooses the path of that connected discharge through clear air or an available conductive route.
- **Anchor:** Hold an exposed fingertip in the intended outlet direction to establish control. Maintain an unbroken line of delivery to direct the discharge out to Close / Room; covering the outlet, breaking the path, or releasing the discharge ends control. A discharge that enters a conductive route follows that route naturally after release.
- **Mark:** Fine blue-white branching lines run beneath the skin of the fingertips and jump between them as the Anchor engages.
- **Boundary:** Cannot control existing electricity, operate or disable electronics, create a persistent conductor, or directly control a living body's nerves or muscles. It grants no insulation or lightning immunity; a returned current, a bad route, or a conductive hold can harm the user.

## Arc Needle

Keywords: Generate + Redirect : Project : Burst : Bolt : Palm-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Generate a narrow discharge from an exposed fingertip and redirect it along a clear path to one exposed target.

**Output:** Deliver an electrical shock to the struck target; the damage and disabling effect remain unresolved.

**Mastery benefit:** When a visible conductive object on the target provides the most direct available route, choose that object as the discharge's contact point. This does not let the arc turn around cover or seek an unseen target.

**Requirement / interruption:** An exposed fingertip, a clear delivery path, and a route that will not carry the discharge back through the user. Covering the outlet, spoiling the aim, or breaking the path disrupts the shot.

**Aftermath:** The arc expires on release. Any heat, spark, or damage at the contact point remains as an ordinary consequence.

## Live Conductor

Keywords: Generate + Redirect : Ward : Sustain : Coating : Custom (held conductor) : Self / Touch

**Method:** Feed a controlled current through a held nonliving conductive object, directing it away from the insulated or otherwise safe grip.

**Output:** Make exposed portions of the object hazardous to touch while the current is sustained. It does not make the object a shield or prevent an attack that avoids the live portion.

**Mastery benefit:** Keep the current confined to a predeclared exposed section of the held object, allowing the user to leave another reachable section safe to handle.

**Requirement / interruption:** A suitable conductive object, a grip protected by its ordinary construction, and the technique's recorded **Energy/min** upkeep. Losing the object, exposing the user to the current, or disrupting the safe route ends the effect and may cause ordinary electrical harm.

**Aftermath:** The object returns to its ordinary electrical state when upkeep ends. It does not remain charged or affect connected devices after the technique ends.
