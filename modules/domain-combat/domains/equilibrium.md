# Equilibrium

[All Domain sets](README.md) | [Combined catalogue](../starter-domains.md) | [Domain Combat](../README.md)

This set contains a draft Domain and two suggested Mastered techniques. They may fill a character's two free starting choices or be adapted with the GM.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses one or two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. Any continuing Energy cost uses a recorded **Energy/min** rate; numerical rates, storage costs and capacity, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## Domain

- **Name:** Equilibrium
- **Origin:** Internal Energy
- **Aspect:**
  - **Keyword:** `Ambient Energy`
  - **Explanation:** Incoming sound, light, and heat in their existing energy forms.
- **Expressions:**
  - **Keywords:** `Absorb` + `Release`
  - **Absorb:** *(Operation Keyword)* Takes energy through the mouth into a Bank.
  - **Release:** *(Operation Keyword)* Discharges stored energy through the mouth, permitting shaping at the outlet without later steering.
- **Anchor:**
  - **Keywords:** `Mouth` (Medium) · `Self / Touch` to `Variable` (Range Bands)
  - **Establish:** The mouth must receive or emit the energy.
  - **Reach:** Limited to energy physically reaching the mouth opening upon intake; discharge range follows the release method.
  - **Maintain:** Keep the mouth open during transfer; closing it prevents transfer while already banked energy remains stored.
  - **Break:** Closing or obstructing the mouth, or reaching Bank capacity ends intake.
- **Mark:** A ring around the lips continues down the throat; it ripples with sound, glows with light, or turns ember-red with heat.
- **Boundary:** The Bank holds one energy type at a time. Exceeding its capacity causes Strain; capacity, retention, and loss remain open. Energy cannot be amplified, converted, or spent as Internal Energy. Unabsorbed portions of a threat can still harm the user.

## Dragon Reversal

- **Keywords:** `Absorb + Release` (Expressions) : `Project` (Form) : `Burst` (Flow) : `Custom (chosen discharge)` (Shape) : `Custom (received envelope)` (Size) : `Custom (received maximum)` (Range)

**Method:** Receive a compatible incoming effect through the mouth, then discharge the entire Bank in the same motion. Agree on the received effect's size and maximum delivery range before resolution.

**Output:** Return the absorbed energy in its original type, ordinarily following the incoming delivery pattern. Existing compatible reserves contribute only their stored energy; they grant no automatic range or size increase.

**Mastery benefit:** Change the discharge's shape within its approved envelope while preserving the original maximum range. Redistribution cannot duplicate energy or preserve full intensity everywhere.

**Requirement / interruption:** An empty Bank or one holding the same type, sufficient capacity, and a successful interception. A blocked mouth or failed interception prevents the reversal.

**Aftermath:** The Bank is empty after a completed discharge. Released energy is uncontrolled; energy already absorbed before an interrupted release remains subject to the Bank rules.

## Hearth Breath

- **Keywords:** `Release` (Expressions) : `Shape` (Form) : `Burst` (Flow) : `Stream` (Shape) : `Palm-sized` (Size) : `Self / Touch` (Range)

**Method:** Exhale a controlled portion of stored heat onto a held object.

**Output:** Warm or thaw the exposed portion according to the available heat and the material.

**Mastery benefit:** Meter the discharge to an agreed warmth and stop before overheating the selected surface, provided its condition is observable.

**Requirement / interruption:** Heat in the Bank and access to the object's surface. Moving the object or blocking the mouth interrupts delivery.

**Aftermath:** Delivered heat stays in the surroundings; undischarged heat remains banked. This neither heals tissue nor grants fire resistance.
