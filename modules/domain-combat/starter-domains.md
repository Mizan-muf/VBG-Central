# Starter Domains and techniques

Each Domain and its two techniques are also available as an [individual set](domains/README.md). Keep the individual sets and this combined catalogue in sync when editing.

Eighteen suggested Domains for [Domain Combat](README.md), each with two Mastered techniques that can fill a character's two free starting choices. Players and the GM may adapt them before play. These are draft permission sets and qualitative outputs, not numerically balanced combat profiles.

All have **Origin: Internal Energy**. Each listed technique uses one or two Expression applications, so its **base execution cost is 1 Internal Energy**. All continuing Energy costs use a recorded **Energy/min** rate. Numerical upkeep rates, storage costs and capacity, damage, protection, and control strength remain unresolved. Choosing one technique does not grant the other for free beyond the two starting choices.

Keyword order: **Expressions : Form : Flow : Shape : Size : Range**. Use `+` within Expressions. Custom shapes and relational sizes or ranges are explicitly defined in their methods; they do not grant unrestricted choices.

Marks are bodily signs of an Anchor engaging. Their locations and appearances below are suggestions. They do not grant immunity, extra senses, or a new way to interrupt the Domain. Users have ordinary bodily tolerance unless the Domain explicitly says otherwise.

## 1. Air

- **Name:** Air
- **Origin:** Internal Energy
- **Aspect:**
  - **Keyword:** `Air Currents`
  - **Explanation:** Existing air currents and pressure differences.
- **Expressions:**
  - **Keywords:** `Compress` + `Redirect`
  - **Compress:** *(Operation Keyword)* Packs air into a smaller space.
  - **Redirect:** *(Operation Keyword)* Changes the direction of an existing current, including one produced by releasing compressed air.
- **Anchor:**
  - **Keywords:** `Breath` (Medium) · `Close / Room` (Range Band)
  - **Establish:** Breath establishes influence beside the mouth.
  - **Reach:** Connected airflow controls it out to Close / Room.
  - **Maintain:** Maintain an uninterrupted breath and connected airflow.
  - **Break:** Breaking either breath or airflow ends control; released air continues naturally.
- **Mark:** Curved lines around the lips and throat ripple outward during controlled breathing.
- **Boundary:** Cannot generate air, affect air inside a living body, or control weather. Pressure and recoil can harm the user.

### Air Pistol

- **Keywords:** `Compress + Redirect` (Expressions) : `Project` (Form) : `Burst` (Flow) : `Bolt` (Shape) : `Palm-sized` (Size) : `Close / Room` (Range)

**Method:** Compress air at the mouth and redirect its release into a narrow shot along a clear path.

**Output:** Strike one exposed target with a blunt pressure impact.

**Mastery benefit:** The impact also pushes a loose, lightly held object from the struck point when the delivered force can dislodge it; it does not guarantee a disarm.

**Requirement / interruption:** A usable breath and clear outlet. Disrupting the breath before release spoils the shot.

**Aftermath:** The shot disperses and cannot be steered after release.

### Crosswind Guard

- **Keywords:** `Redirect` (Expressions) : `Ward` (Form) : `Sustain` (Flow) : `Wall` (Shape) : `Person-sized` (Size) : `Melee / Reach` (Range)

**Method:** Turn an existing current into a crosswind across the front of the body.

**Output:** Deflect loose dust, smoke, and light airborne debris that the current can move.

**Mastery benefit:** Leave a controlled viewing slit through the moving screen.

**Requirement / interruption:** Enough existing airflow for the threat; a still room does not supply a strong defensive wind. Broken breath or disconnected airflow ends the screen.

**Aftermath:** Displaced matter remains wherever ordinary airflow carries it. This is not a solid shield.

## 2. Flame

- **Name:** Flame
- **Origin:** Internal Energy
- **Aspect:**
  - **Keyword:** `Flame`
  - **Explanation:** Flame generated from the user's hands.
- **Expressions:**
  - **Keywords:** `Generate` + `Shape`
  - **Generate:** *(Operation Keyword)* Produces flame at a palm from Internal Energy.
  - **Shape:** *(Operation Keyword)* Arranges that flame while it remains connected to the palm.
- **Anchor:**
  - **Keywords:** `Open Palm` (Medium) · `Close / Room` (Range Band)
  - **Establish:** An open palm begins connected flame.
  - **Reach:** Up to Close / Room while maintaining physical connection.
  - **Maintain:** Keep the palm open and the flame connected.
  - **Break:** Closing the palm or severing the flame's connection ends control.
- **Mark:** Ember-like spirals across the palms brighten from the centre during generation.
- **Boundary:** Cannot control unrelated fire or separated flame, create fuel, or grant heat immunity. Unsupported magical flame expires when its supply ends; ignited fuel can keep burning naturally.

### Furnace Palm

- **Keywords:** `Generate + Shape` (Expressions) : `Project` (Form) : `Burst` (Flow) : `Stream` (Shape) : `Palm-sized` (Size) : `Close / Room` (Range)

**Method:** Release a palm-thick jet in a chosen direction, shaping it while connected to the hand.

**Output:** Deal fire damage to the exposed target along the jet's path; the damage value remains open.

**Mastery benefit:** Concentrate the jet for increased damage at its point of impact; the increase remains open.

**Requirement / interruption:** An open palm, a clear path, and a release the body can tolerate. Spoiling the hand's aim or closing it before release disrupts delivery.

**Aftermath:** Control ends with the release. Combustible targets may remain alight through ordinary burning.

### Burning Screen

- **Keywords:** `Generate + Shape` (Expressions) : `Veil` (Form) : `Sustain` (Flow) : `Wall` (Shape) : `Person-sized` (Size) : `Melee / Reach` (Range)

**Method:** Raise a connected sheet of flame from the palm between the user and an observer.

**Output:** Glare and moving flame obscure the user's outline from the screened direction.

**Mastery benefit:** Shape a narrow sight gap at the user's eye level.

**Requirement / interruption:** Space for the sheet and an open palm. Losing its connection ends the screen. It does not stop weapons simply because it resembles a wall.

**Aftermath:** The magical sheet expires; any fires it starts remain hazards to everyone.

## 3. Biomass

- **Name:** Biomass
- **Origin:** Internal Energy
- **Aspect:**
  - **Keyword:** `Biomass`
  - **Explanation:** Inert, nonliving organic biological matter generated from Internal Energy.
- **Expressions:**
  - **Keywords:** `Generate` + `Shape`
  - **Generate:** *(Operation Keyword)* Creates inert biomass from Internal Energy.
  - **Shape:** *(Operation Keyword)* Arranges biomass generated by this Domain while it remains connected to the palm.
- **Anchor:**
  - **Keywords:** `Open Palm` (Medium) · `Melee / Reach` (Range Band)
  - **Establish:** An open palm establishes generation and shaping out to Melee / Reach.
  - **Reach:** Up to Melee / Reach while maintaining physical connection.
  - **Maintain:** Keep an unbroken physical connection to the palm and pay the technique's recorded Sustain upkeep.
  - **Break:** A severed physical connection ends active control over that portion; ending upkeep causes the biomass to dissolve.
- **Mark:** Branching lines across the palms extend towards the wrists when biomass is generated or shaped.
- **Boundary:** Generated biomass is nonliving. It grants no healing, Energy recovery, copied abilities, senses, control of other bodies, or immunity to harmful material. It persists only while its recorded Sustain **Energy/min** upkeep is paid.

### Borrowed Grip

- **Keywords:** `Generate + Shape` (Expressions) : `Bind` (Form) : `Sustain` (Flow) : `Custom (tendril)` (Shape) : `Custom (arm-thick)` (Size) : `Melee / Reach` (Range)

**Method:** Generate and shape fibrous biomass from an open palm into a tendril that wraps around a reachable limb or object.

**Output:** Restrain the caught part against the tendril's agreed strength.

**Mastery benefit:** Maintain a precise grip on a small handle or moving joint without crushing it.

**Requirement / interruption:** An open palm establishes the tendril and its recorded **Energy/min** upkeep sustains it. Losing its connection, severing it, or overcoming its grip defeats the active restraint.

**Aftermath:** The tendril dissolves when upkeep ends. A disconnected but sustained tendril remains physical but cannot continue tightening afterward.

### Grafted Buckler

- **Keywords:** `Generate + Shape` (Expressions) : `Ward` (Form) : `Sustain` (Flow) : `Custom (buckler)` (Shape) : `Custom (forearm-sized)` (Size) : `Self / Touch` (Range)

**Method:** Generate and shape layered biomass from an open palm into a shield attached to the forearm.

**Output:** Create a physical guard whose protection follows its recorded material properties.

**Mastery benefit:** Shape a fitted wrist brace that helps keep the shield aligned during an impact it can withstand.

**Requirement / interruption:** An open palm establishes the guard and its recorded **Energy/min** upkeep sustains it. Disrupting its form or stopping upkeep ends its protection.

**Aftermath:** The buckler dissolves when upkeep ends. Damage stays damage; its formation does not restore Strain.

## 4. Equilibrium

- **Name:** Equilibrium
- **Origin:** Internal Energy
- **Aspect:**
  - **Keyword:** `Ambient Energy`
  - **Explanation:** Incoming sound, light, and heat in their existing energy forms.
- **Expressions:**
  - **Keywords:** `Absorb` + `Release`
  - **Absorb:** *(Operation Keyword)* Takes energy through the mouth into a Bank.
  - **Release:** *(Operation Keyword)* Discharges stored energy through the mouth, permitting shaping at the outlet without later steering.
- **Anchor:**
  - **Keywords:** `Mouth` (Medium) · `Self / Touch` to `Variable` (Range Bands)
  - **Establish:** The mouth must receive or emit the energy.
  - **Reach:** Limited to energy physically reaching the mouth opening upon intake; discharge range follows the release method.
  - **Maintain:** Keep the mouth open during transfer; closing it prevents transfer while already banked energy remains stored.
  - **Break:** Closing or obstructing the mouth, or reaching Bank capacity ends intake.
- **Mark:** A ring around the lips continues down the throat; it ripples with sound, glows with light, or turns ember-red with heat.
- **Boundary:** The Bank holds one energy type at a time. Exceeding its capacity causes Strain; capacity, retention, and loss remain open. Energy cannot be amplified, converted, or spent as Internal Energy. Unabsorbed portions of a threat can still harm the user.

### Dragon Reversal

- **Keywords:** `Absorb + Release` (Expressions) : `Project` (Form) : `Burst` (Flow) : `Custom (chosen discharge)` (Shape) : `Custom (received envelope)` (Size) : `Custom (received maximum)` (Range)

**Method:** Receive a compatible incoming effect through the mouth, then discharge the entire Bank in the same motion. Agree on the received effect's size and maximum delivery range before resolution.

**Output:** Return the absorbed energy in its original type, ordinarily following the incoming delivery pattern. Existing compatible reserves contribute only their stored energy; they grant no automatic range or size increase.

**Mastery benefit:** Change the discharge's shape within its approved envelope while preserving the original maximum range. Redistribution cannot duplicate energy or preserve full intensity everywhere.

**Requirement / interruption:** An empty Bank or one holding the same type, sufficient capacity, and a successful interception. A blocked mouth or failed interception prevents the reversal.

**Aftermath:** The Bank is empty after a completed discharge. Released energy is uncontrolled; energy already absorbed before an interrupted release remains subject to the Bank rules.

### Hearth Breath

- **Keywords:** `Release` (Expressions) : `Shape` (Form) : `Burst` (Flow) : `Stream` (Shape) : `Palm-sized` (Size) : `Self / Touch` (Range)

**Method:** Exhale a controlled portion of stored heat onto a held object.

**Output:** Warm or thaw the exposed portion according to the available heat and the material.

**Mastery benefit:** Meter the discharge to an agreed warmth and stop before overheating the selected surface, provided its condition is observable.

**Requirement / interruption:** Heat in the Bank and access to the object's surface. Moving the object or blocking the mouth interrupts delivery.

**Aftermath:** Delivered heat stays in the surroundings; undischarged heat remains banked. This neither heals tissue nor grants fire resistance.

## 5. Ink

- **Name:** Ink
- **Origin:** Internal Energy
- **Aspect:**
  - **Keyword:** `Written Ink`
  - **Explanation:** Ink marks personally written by the user.
- **Expressions:**
  - **Keywords:** `Spread` + `Gather`
  - **Spread:** *(Operation Keyword)* Moves marked ink across connected surfaces.
  - **Gather:** *(Operation Keyword)* Draws it back along those surfaces into a selected mark.
- **Anchor:**
  - **Keywords:** `Handwriting` (Medium) · `Close / Room` (Range Band)
  - **Establish:** Write the initial mark by hand.
  - **Reach:** Up to Close / Room with line of sight.
  - **Maintain:** Maintain sight of the affected ink within Close / Room across a connected surface.
  - **Break:** Losing sight or range ends motion.
- **Mark:** Dark whorls on the writing fingertips flow like wet ink while the Anchor is active.
- **Boundary:** Cannot create ink, move it through open air, read hidden information, or affect another person's writing. Displaced ink remains where left and may dry or stain naturally.

### Blackout Script

- **Keywords:** `Spread` (Expressions) : `Veil` (Form) : `Sustain` (Flow) : `Coating` (Shape) : `Person-sized` (Size) : `Close / Room` (Range)

**Method:** Spread a personally written mark over a connected window or written surface.

**Output:** Obscure the covered portion where the ink is thick enough to be opaque.

**Mastery benefit:** Preserve selected visible letters or a narrow viewing opening within the coating.

**Requirement / interruption:** Enough marked ink, a traversable surface, and sight. Washing away ink removes coverage; losing the Anchor stops adjustment.

**Aftermath:** The coating remains physically present until gathered, cleaned, or otherwise removed. Sustain pays for active adjustment, not the continued existence of a stain.

### Returning Cipher

- **Keywords:** `Gather` (Expressions) : `Shape` (Form) : `Burst` (Flow) : `Custom (written mark)` (Shape) : `Palm-sized` (Size) : `Close / Room` (Range)

**Method:** Draw the ink of a personally written message back into a compact mark on the same connected surface.

**Output:** Remove the readable arrangement of that message while retaining its ink.

**Mastery benefit:** Leave a selected signature or symbol intact during gathering.

**Requirement / interruption:** Visible, movable marked ink. Absorbed stains or impressions in the surface cannot be erased by moving the remaining ink.

**Aftermath:** The collected ink remains; the technique does not hide scratches, witnesses, or copies of the message.

## 6. Glass

- **Aspect:** Broken, nonliving glass personally touched by the user.
- **Expressions:** Gather draws eligible pieces together through visible space; Shape arranges and holds rigid fragments in a chosen structure without fusing them.
- **Anchor:** Touch each piece to establish eligibility. Active control requires sight within Close / Room; losing either sight or range releases that piece. Touch alone does not provide remote sensing.
- **Mark:** Faceted lines cross the fingertips and catch the light when pieces are gathered.
- **Boundary:** Cannot break intact glass, generate it, soften it, or grant cut resistance. Unsupported arrangements fall when control ends; pieces remain ordinary glass.

### Shard Canopy

Keywords: Gather + Shape : Ward : Sustain : Wall : Person-sized : Melee / Reach

**Method:** Gather touched shards into a tilted screen in front of the user.

**Output:** Interpose brittle material against threats it can physically block or deflect.

**Mastery benefit:** Maintain a narrow viewing gap while repositioning the surrounding fragments.

**Requirement / interruption:** Enough eligible glass and an unobstructed gathering route. Broken sight releases affected shards; a strong impact may shatter or scatter them.

**Aftermath:** Released shards fall and remain a hazard.

### Mirror Mosaic

Keywords: Gather + Shape : Shape : Sustain : Custom (reflective panel) : Palm-sized : Self / Touch

**Method:** Assemble touched, reflective fragments into a small panel above the palm.

**Output:** Provide an ordinary reflected view from the panel's chosen angle.

**Mastery benefit:** Align the fragments into a steadier, readable image instead of separate disjointed reflections.

**Requirement / interruption:** Suitable reflective fragments and direct sight of the assembly. The view still depends on lighting and the reflection's physical path.

**Aftermath:** The assembly separates when released. It grants no vision through opaque objects.

## 7. Knots

- **Aspect:** Knots personally tied in flexible material.
- **Expressions:** Tighten cinches an existing knot; Loosen relaxes it, including fully untying it.
- **Anchor:** Tie the knot through touch. Control remains possible within Close / Room while the knot is intact; it grants no remote perception. Cutting or fully untying it ends the connection.
- **Mark:** Interlaced lines on the fingers draw taut during tightening and relax during loosening.
- **Boundary:** Cannot create rope, tie new knots remotely, move an entire cord independently, or affect someone else's knots. Adjusted knots remain physically tied after control ends.

### Snarestep

Keywords: Tighten : Bind : Burst : Custom (loop) : Custom (limb-sized) : Close / Room

**Method:** Physically cast a prepared knotted loop around a limb or object, then cinch it.

**Output:** Catch and hinder what the loop successfully encloses, within the cord's strength.

**Mastery benefit:** Use a practised trigger to cinch at the visible moment of contact, reducing premature closure.

**Requirement / interruption:** A throwable cord and a visible target. A miss, cut cord, or broken knot prevents restraint; the cord does not guide itself.

**Aftermath:** The knot remains tied until loosened, cut, or physically overcome.

### Slipknot Exit

Keywords: Loosen : Shift : Burst : Custom (knot) : Palm-sized : Self / Touch

**Method:** Loosen a personally tied fastening securing the user's cloak, harness, or pack.

**Output:** Free the user from that fastening so the caught equipment can be shed.

**Mastery benefit:** Stop at a prepared loose configuration that can be physically fastened again without rethreading the whole assembly.

**Requirement / interruption:** The fastening must be the user's own intact knot. Other buckles, restraints, or an enemy holding the body still need separate answers.

**Aftermath:** Released equipment stays where it falls. Fully untying the knot ends its Anchor.

## 8. Echoes

- **Aspect:** Sounds personally captured by the user.
- **Expressions:** Capture stores a sound reaching a cupped hand; Release plays it from that hand in a chosen direction. Capture does not silence the sound for other listeners.
- **Anchor:** Cup a hand to capture, then close it to retain the echo. Open it deliberately to release; forcing it open disperses the stored sound. Directed delivery reaches up to Close / Room, subject to acoustics and obstructions.
- **Mark:** Concentric rings in the palms contract around a held echo and spread during release.
- **Boundary:** Cannot invent words, amplify captured sound, reproduce supernatural powers, or place a sound source remotely. A captured passage is spent when released; storage limits and retention remain open.

### Borrowed Command

Keywords: Capture + Release : Veil : Burst : Cone : Person-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Catch a short spoken phrase and immediately play it towards a listener from a concealed hand. The cone is person-wide at its far end.

**Output:** Reproduce the captured voice and words, potentially misleading someone about who spoke nearby.

**Mastery benefit:** Preserve clear diction and cadence through the directional release.

**Requirement / interruption:** A suitable audible phrase and a concealed outlet if deception requires it. Listeners can recognise the trick; the phrase is not a command they must obey.

**Aftermath:** The echo is spent and travels as ordinary sound. Interrupting the hand before release spoils delivery.

### Corner Knock

Keywords: Release : Veil : Burst : Cone : Palm-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Direct a previously captured knock towards a nearby hard surface so its reflection draws attention there. The narrow cone is palm-wide at that surface.

**Output:** Produce an audible distraction whose apparent direction depends on the room's acoustics.

**Mastery benefit:** Aim the reflection at a chosen visible surface with greater directional precision.

**Requirement / interruption:** A stored knock, an open delivery path, and a useful reflecting surface. The base cost covers release; obtaining and retaining the echo is separate.

**Aftermath:** The echo is spent. The sound originates at the hand and reflects naturally; it does not teleport behind cover.

## 9. Friction

- **Aspect:** Friction between nonliving contacting surfaces.
- **Expressions:** Increase raises resistance to sliding; Reduce lowers it.
- **Anchor:** Establish influence through bodily contact with one surface at the affected interface, including worn footwear or gloves. Only the local contact patch is affected. Losing that connection ends the change immediately.
- **Mark:** Ridges across the palms and soles sharpen or smooth visually as friction changes.
- **Boundary:** Cannot propel an object without force, create adhesion across a gap, strengthen materials, or protect against falls and sudden stops. Ordinary friction returns when control ends.

### Lockstep

Keywords: Reduce + Increase : Shift : Sustain : Coating : Custom (footwear contact) : Self / Touch

**Method:** Lower friction beneath the footwear while pushing off, then raise it progressively to brake at a declared destination within Close.

**Output:** Slide along a continuous clear surface using the user's momentum. Close is the movement limit, not remote control range.

**Mastery benefit:** Finish in a controlled stance when the available braking distance and surface can safely absorb the momentum.

**Requirement / interruption:** Ground contact, a usable route, and an initial push. Leaving the surface or losing footing interrupts controlled travel; the technique cannot cancel excessive momentum safely.

**Aftermath:** Ordinary friction resumes. A jump does not carry the effect through the air.

### Surehold

Keywords: Increase : Ward : Sustain : Coating : Palm-sized : Self / Touch

**Method:** Increase friction between worn gloves and a held nonliving object.

**Output:** Resist the object slipping or being pulled through the grip.

**Mastery benefit:** Keep the friction change confined to the intended gripping patches, leaving nearby contacts free to move.

**Requirement / interruption:** Gloves, sustained contact, and a grip the body can support. Losing contact ends the effect; excessive force can still move or injure the user.

**Aftermath:** No adhesion remains after release.

## 10. Shadows

- **Aspect:** Shadows cast by the user's body.
- **Expressions:** Extend stretches a connected cast shadow; Distort alters its outline on a receiving surface.
- **Anchor:** The body must interrupt a visible light source. Maintain that relationship and a continuous cast shadow on surfaces within Close / Room. Removing the source, blocking its path differently, or breaking the shadow's connection ends the affected extension.
- **Mark:** Dark tracery lies over the skin currently casting the controlled silhouette; it shifts across the body as the lighting changes.
- **Boundary:** Cannot create independent darkness, affect another creature's shadow, make solid objects, or produce coloured and detailed illusions. Shadows return to their natural shapes when control ends.

### Crooked Silhouette

Keywords: Extend + Distort : Veil : Sustain : Coating : Person-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Stretch the user's shadow onto a nearby wall and alter its outline into a chosen silhouette.

**Output:** Misrepresent the posture or position of the body casting it to observers relying on the shadow.

**Mastery benefit:** Maintain a recognisable predeclared outline, such as a hanging coat, through small ordinary movements of the user.

**Requirement / interruption:** A useful light source and receiving surface. Seeing the actual body may defeat the deception; losing the cast connection ends it.

**Aftermath:** The ordinary silhouette returns. The user's body never becomes invisible.

### Shadow Script

Keywords: Extend + Distort : Shape : Sustain : Coating : Person-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Stretch the connected shadow onto a surface and distort its edge into letters, arrows, or simple symbols.

**Output:** Display a silent message visible to anyone with a clear view of that surface.

**Mastery benefit:** Keep the lettering legible on an uneven surface by compensating for its visible contours.

**Requirement / interruption:** A connected cast shadow and a surface with sufficient contrast. Disrupting the light or connection erases the message.

**Aftermath:** No writing remains once the effect ends.

## 11. Electricity

- **Aspect:** Electrical discharges generated at the user's fingertips.
- **Expressions:** Generate produces a discharge at an exposed fingertip; Redirect chooses the path of that connected discharge through clear air or an available conductive route.
- **Anchor:** Hold an exposed fingertip in the intended outlet direction to establish control. Maintain an unbroken line of delivery to direct the discharge out to Close / Room; covering the outlet, breaking the path, or releasing the discharge ends control. A discharge that enters a conductive route follows that route naturally after release.
- **Mark:** Fine blue-white branching lines run beneath the skin of the fingertips and jump between them as the Anchor engages.
- **Boundary:** Cannot control existing electricity, operate or disable electronics, create a persistent conductor, or directly control a living body's nerves or muscles. It grants no insulation or lightning immunity; a returned current, a bad route, or a conductive hold can harm the user.

### Arc Needle

Keywords: Generate + Redirect : Project : Burst : Bolt : Palm-sized : Close / Room

**Method:** Generate a narrow discharge from an exposed fingertip and redirect it along a clear path to one exposed target.

**Output:** Deliver an electrical shock to the struck target; the damage and disabling effect remain unresolved.

**Mastery benefit:** When a visible conductive object on the target provides the most direct available route, choose that object as the discharge's contact point. This does not let the arc turn around cover or seek an unseen target.

**Requirement / interruption:** An exposed fingertip, a clear delivery path, and a route that will not carry the discharge back through the user. Covering the outlet, spoiling the aim, or breaking the path disrupts the shot.

**Aftermath:** The arc expires on release. Any heat, spark, or damage at the contact point remains as an ordinary consequence.

### Live Conductor

Keywords: Generate + Redirect : Ward : Sustain : Coating : Custom (held conductor) : Self / Touch

**Method:** Feed a controlled current through a held nonliving conductive object, directing it away from the insulated or otherwise safe grip.

**Output:** Make exposed portions of the object hazardous to touch while the current is sustained. It does not make the object a shield or prevent an attack that avoids the live portion.

**Mastery benefit:** Keep the current confined to a predeclared exposed section of the held object, allowing the user to leave another reachable section safe to handle.

**Requirement / interruption:** A suitable conductive object, a grip protected by its ordinary construction, and the technique's recorded **Energy/min** upkeep. Losing the object, exposing the user to the current, or disrupting the safe route ends the effect and may cause ordinary electrical harm.

**Aftermath:** The object returns to its ordinary electrical state when upkeep ends. It does not remain charged or affect connected devices after the technique ends.

## 12. Scales

- **Aspect:** Protective keratinous scales generated over the user's skin.
- **Expressions:** Generate creates inert overlapping scales at an exposed patch of the user's skin; Shape arranges scales generated by this Domain while they remain connected to that patch.
- **Anchor:** Touch the exposed skin where scales emerge to establish their formation. Maintain that contact and an unbroken physical connection to shape them over the user's body out to Self / Touch; covering, losing contact with, or detaching the scales ends active control.
- **Mark:** Iridescent, scale-like lines spread from the contacted patch and lie flat again when the Anchor releases.
- **Boundary:** Scales protect only where they physically cover and only according to their agreed material strength. They cannot heal, replace lost tissue, grant resistance to heat, poison, or impact beyond their physical coverage, cover another creature, or move independently. Detached scales are inert and dissolve when upkeep ends.

### Scale Mantle

Keywords: Generate + Shape : Ward : Sustain : Coating : Person-sized : Self / Touch

**Method:** Generate overlapping scales from an exposed patch and shape them into a close-fitted mantle over the torso and one declared limb.

**Output:** Create a physical protective layer against threats it can physically cover and withstand.

**Mastery benefit:** Leave one chosen joint flexible enough for ordinary movement without opening a gap larger than the technique's normal overlap.

**Requirement / interruption:** An exposed anchor patch and the technique's recorded **Energy/min** upkeep. Losing contact with the anchor patch, breaking the connected layer, or overwhelming its material strength defeats protection at the affected point.

**Aftermath:** The scales dissolve when upkeep ends. Damage already reaching the body remains damage.

### Razor Gauntlet

Keywords: Generate + Shape : Shape : Sustain : Custom (bladed gauntlet) : Custom (hand and forearm) : Self / Touch

**Method:** Generate scales from the hand and shape their outer edges into a gauntlet of overlapping ridges.

**Output:** Give the covered hand and forearm a physical cutting or tearing surface for a normal melee strike or tool use.

**Mastery benefit:** Fold the ridges flat over the palm while keeping a declared outer edge exposed, allowing the user to maintain an ordinary grip on an appropriate handle.

**Requirement / interruption:** An exposed hand, an intact connection to the anchor patch, and the technique's recorded **Energy/min** upkeep. A blocked hand, disrupted scales, or an impact that breaks the ridges spoils the enhanced edge.

**Aftermath:** The gauntlet dissolves when upkeep ends. It does not sharpen held weapons, guide an attack, or guarantee that a strike penetrates protection.

## 13. Drive

- **Aspect:** The user's own physical exertion, stored as a finite Drive Bank.
- **Expressions:** Accumulate stores exertion produced by the user's deliberate braced effort in the Drive Bank; Release delivers the stored exertion through one continuous physical effort made by the user's body or a held object.
- **Anchor:** Plant both feet against a supporting surface and press a marked palm to the sternum to establish a Bank. Maintain the braced stance to accumulate. Once charged, keep the marked hand closed around the intended limb or held object's grip to retain the Bank; opening the hand, losing the object, or releasing force through another route empties it.
- **Mark:** A dark spiral forms under the sternum-side palm and tightens toward its centre as the Bank fills.
- **Boundary:** Cannot store another creature's force, create motion without a physical effort, propel a released object, or direct force at range. The Bank cannot exceed its agreed capacity or reinforce a body against harm; excessive exertion, recoil, an unsafe landing, or a force the body cannot bear can still cause ordinary injury.

### Brace and Bank

Keywords: Accumulate : Ward : Accumulate : Coating : Person-sized : Self

**Method:** Take a stable, load-bearing stance with the marked palm pressed to the sternum, then deliberately tense and exert against the support until the declared Drive Bank is charged.

**Output:** Store a declared amount of the user's own exertion for one later Release, within the Bank's agreed capacity.

**Mastery benefit:** Halt accumulation at a declared partial charge without losing the stored exertion, provided the Anchor remains intact.

**Requirement / interruption:** Both feet need a surface that can take the load, and the user must remain braced with the palm against the sternum. Being forced off balance, losing the support, or breaking the Anchor spoils the accumulation; already stored exertion follows the Domain's Bank rules.

**Aftermath:** The Bank remains only while its retention condition holds. It does not make the user stronger, heavier, or harder to move before Release.

### Loaded Blow

## 15. Hellion

Illustration cards: [Dreadnought Flesh](art/hellion/dreadnought-flesh.png) · [Ruin Drive](art/hellion/ruin-drive.png). [Art prompts](art/hellion/README.md).

A second spine rises beneath the skin. Extra ribs crawl around the chest, muscle cords wind around the limbs, and pale plates close over the face like a second jaw. When the user moves, the armor parts along wet seams and the anatomy underneath pulls them forward.

Hellion turns the body into a hulking siege creature. Its visual identity is an ivory cage over bruised violet muscle, with tooth-like plate edges and a raised vertebral ridge. The transformation feels like something inside the user unfolding into its adult shape.

### Domain

- **Origin:** Internal Energy.

  - **Articulate:** Arrange and contract the generated anatomy around the user's body: seat its plates, position its supports, and drive its muscles in concert with the user's movement. This does not change its material properties.
- **Anchor:**
  - **Establish:** Send a deliberate pulse of Internal Energy through the mark along the spine; growth begins beneath the skin of the back.
  - **Reach:** Self. The anatomy must remain attached to the back and fitted around the user's body.
  - **Maintain:** Remain conscious and keep the generated anatomy physically connected to its root at the back. No touch, exposed skin, fixed posture, or continuous back tension is required.
  - **Break:** Voluntary release, unconsciousness, or destruction of the generated root ends the connection. Severing a section disconnects only that section. An ordinary blow to the back does not automatically break the Anchor.
- **Boundary:** Creates one attached bodily frame, within the size and output recorded for the technique. It cannot become a separate creature, extend independent limbs or tendrils, consume bodies, copy traits, or heal the user's injuries. Rigid clothing or equipment can obstruct growth; equipment does not transform with the user.
- **Tolerance:** The user can endure the anatomy's emergence, attachment, and ordinary contraction without losing Strain merely for transforming. Its frame supports its own weight and routes its approved muscular force through reinforced joints. Protection comes from actual coverage and material strength; injury reaching the original body and exertion beyond the recorded output can still reduce Strain. Heat, suffocation, and poison remain threats.
- **Persistence:** Attached anatomy requires its technique's Sustain upkeep. When upkeep or the Anchor ends, it folds toward the back and crumbles into inert flakes that fade without leaving usable material. Disconnected sections immediately become inert and decay the same way. Injuries to the original body remain; reforming damaged armor requires a new paid application of Generate.
- **Mark:** A row of pale, tooth-shaped ridges under the skin follows the spine. On activation they lift in sequence from the lower back to the neck, and dark branching lines spread across the shoulder blades. The mark itself grants no extra weak point.
