# Optional modules

[VBG 2.2](../rules/README.md) is playable without modules. Adopt a module explicitly and use its listed overrides; all other rules remain with the core.

| Module | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Domain Combat](domain-combat/README.md) | Fresh design for player-created techniques, energy flow, storage, and overload | Concept proposal; not yet playable |
| [Arcana Deck](arcana-deck/README.md) | Random Domain decks, ranked cards, combos, daily draws, and HP replacing Strain | Untested design; supporting procedures and prices remain proposals |
| [Charge Combat](charge-combat/README.md) | Separate ability Charge and battle-map movement | Experimental v0.1; untested |
| [VBG-Z](zombie/README.md) | Zombie survival-horror rules and catalogs | Preserved legacy v2.0; not adapted to VBG 2.2 |

Arcana Deck uses cards and HP. Charge Combat uses Charge and Strain. They are alternatives; combining them requires an explicit adapter. Moving modules out of `drafts/` does not approve their unresolved proposals.

Arcana assets: [Domain decks](arcana-deck/domain-decks.md), [constellation](arcana-deck/constellation.svg), and [art prompts and provenance](arcana-deck/art-prompts.md).
