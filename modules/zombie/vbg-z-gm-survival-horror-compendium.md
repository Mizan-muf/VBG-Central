## **1\. SETTING TARGET DIFFICULTIES (TN) & ADJUDICATION**

Tell players the TN or narrative difficulty tier before they roll:  
\* **TN 10 (Routine):** Low-stress action, basic observation, foraging a ransacked room.  
\* **TN 15 (Risky):** Standard combat action, prying a door before zombies arrive, stealthing across broken glass.  
\* **TN 20 (Desperate):** Precision called shot, evading a Licker's tongue strike, leaping across a burning chasm.  
\* **TN 25+ (Extreme):** Staggering a charging Tyrant/Stalker, disarming a biological fail-safe under a 60-second timer.

### **Adjudicating Outcomes**

* **Failure (Miss by 5+):** Deal 1 Strain/Armor damage, trigger an environmental hazard, or tick the Noise Clock by \+1.  
* **Success with Cost (Miss by 1–4):** Award the success, but exact a price: player marks 1 Strain, checks 1 weapon durability box, expends extra ammo, or gains a minor condition.  
* **Clean Success (Beat by 0–5):** Complete objective without secondary penalties.  
* **Critical Success (Beat by 6+):** Perfect feat. Award bonus damage, environmental knockdown, or decay the local Noise Clock by \-1.

## **2\. THE AREA NOISE & THREAT CLOCK ENGINE**

Every active sector or building floor operates on a **0 to 6 Tick Noise Clock**:  
\[NOISE CLOCK\] \[ \]\[ \]\[ \]\[ \]\[ \]\[ \] (0/6 Ticks)

### **Noise Triggers (Increases)**

* \+1 Tick: 9mm Handgun fire, loud physical impacts (kicking wooden doors, shattering windows), failed stealth rolls.  
* \+2 Ticks: 12-Gauge Shotgun blast, Tactical Rifle shot, battering steel security gates.  
* \+3 Ticks: Magnum fire, Hand Grenades, exploding fuel barrels.

### **Clock Decay & Resets**

* \-1 Tick: Completing 1 full Exploration Scene without unsuppressed gunfire.  
* \-1 Tick: Transitioning to a new sector/floor without active pursuit.  
* Isolation: Entering a verified Safe Room (Noise Clock does not advance inside).

### **Escalation Milestones**

* **Ticks 1–3 (Quiet to Noticeable):** Ambient distant groans; wanderers shuffle in adjacent corridors.  
* **Ticks 4–5 (Elevated Risk):** 1–2 shamblers or a mutant investigate the current room. Stealth TNs increase by \+2.  
* **Tick 6/6 (BREACH EVENT):** Immediate ambush\! A horde breaches windows/doors, or a roaming Boss-tier Stalker spawns into the room. The clock resets to 1 Tick after resolving or escaping the breach.

## **3\. ASYNCHRONOUS MONSTER BLUEPRINTS (B.O.W. CARDS)**

*Monsters do not roll dice. They declare incoming threats, forcing players to react using their Major \+ Minor actions.*

### **SHAMBLER HORDE (Threat Tier: Standard)**

* **Description:** Decaying, relentless infected driven by pure viral hunger.  
* **Armor:** ⬜⬜ (2 Boxes total per cluster).  
* **Actions:**  
* *Lunge & Grab:* Forces Finesse or Force TN 15\. Failure results in a bite (+1 Strain, \+1 Virus Infection Stage). Can be countered instantly with a knife durability check or point-blank handgun shot.  
* *Swarm Compression:* Traps a survivor against a wall; requires Force TN 15 to shove free.  
* **Strengths:** Immune to pain and shock; unphased by limb strikes.  
* **Weaknesses:** Fire & Headshots (Called shot TN 20 destroys instantly and prevents V-ACT mutation).  
* **V-ACT Mutation (Crimson Heads):** Any corpse left unburned or undecapitated will revive after 2 scenes as a Crimson Head (Armor ⬜⬜⬜, sprints, attacks twice per turn).

### **LICKER (Threat Tier: Major)**

* **Description:** Blind quadrupedal predator with exposed cerebral tissue, razor talons, and a prehensile barbed tongue.  
* **Armor:** ⬜⬜⬜ (3 Boxes).  
* **Actions:**  
* *Ceiling Ambush / Pounce:* Forces Finesse TN 18 or take 1 Armor/Strain damage and suffer the *Limb Damaged* condition.  
* *Barbed Tongue Lash:* Forces Finesse TN 16 at range. Failure inflicts 1 Strain and the *Bleeding* condition.  
* **Strengths:** Climbs walls/ceilings; incredible acoustic tracking (+2 TN to stealth).  
* **Weaknesses:** Completely blind. Moving at walking pace requires only Finesse TN 10 to slip past completely unnoticed. Highly vulnerable to Shotguns & Acid.

### **THE PURSUER / STALKER (Threat Tier: Boss)**

* **Description:** Hulking, trench-coated bio-engineered tyrant programmed for relentless tracking and eradication.  
* **Armor:** ⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜ (5 Boxes; regenerates 1 box at the end of each combat round).  
* **Actions:**  
* *Choke Slam / Wall Slam:* Forces Force TN 20 to resist. Failure inflicts 2 Strain and the *Panicked* condition.  
* *Relentless Advance:* Smashes through reinforced walls and locked wooden doors as a free environmental action.  
* *Tentacle Sweep:* Forces all players in the room to roll Finesse TN 15 or be knocked prone.  
* **Strengths:** Cannot be permanently killed with standard small arms. Heavy firearms (Shotguns, Magnums) only stagger or delay it.  
* **Weaknesses:** Concentrated heavy explosives or environmental hazards (dropping shipping containers, electrical grids) will force it to kneel for 1 scene, allowing escape.

## **4\. ASYNCHRONOUS SCENE PACING & RESOLUTION**

### **24-Hour Side-Initiative Cycle**

* **GM Scene Post:** Describe the environment, state the active Noise Clock (e.g., 3/6), and declare all incoming hostile threats.  
* **Player Reaction Phase:** Players have 24 hours to post their narrative action, declare 1 Major Action \+ 1 Minor Action, and roll 1d20 \+ Stat \+ Trait. Conditional posting is encouraged (*"If the zombie grabs me, I counter with my knife; otherwise I shoot"*).  
* **GM Resolution Post:** The GM resolves all rolls simultaneously, updates room vitals, applies damage/conditions, ticks the Noise Clock, and sets the next scene.

## **5\. CAMPAIGN PROGRESSION (PP REWARDS & SHOP)**

### **PP Award Guidelines**

* **\+1 PP:** Surviving a hazardous combat encounter or navigating a dangerous trap.  
* **\+2 PP:** Discovering a key research file, solving an environmental puzzle, or securing a safehouse.  
* **\+3 PP:** Completing a major story chapter or defeating a Boss-tier B.O.W.

### **PP Shop**

* **10 PP:** Increase a Stat by \+1 (Force, Finesse, or Focus; maximum \+3).  
* **15 PP:** Learn a new Minor Trait (+1 Bonus).  
* **20 PP:** Unlock a new Tactical Specialization Domain.  
* **25 PP:** Gain \+1 Permanent Inventory Slot (Maximum 6 slots).

### **Strict Healing Economy (Herbs & FAS Only)**

* **No Natural Strain Recovery:** Safe room rests and downtime scenes do NOT restore Strain boxes.  
* **Strict Medical Items (Herbs & First Aid Spray Only):** In VBG-Z survival horror, all medical recovery, Strain healing, and condition treatment is strictly limited to **First Aid Sprays** and **Medicinal Herbs** (Green, Red, Blue, and their compounds). There are NO bandages, suture kits, or medical dressings in the game.
