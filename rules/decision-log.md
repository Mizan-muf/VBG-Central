# Very Basic RPG 2.2 — Decision Log

**Status:** current decision record · **Version:** 2.2 · **Revised:** 2026-09-29<br>
**Owns:** unresolved rules, editorial status, and the record of changes to this document set.

> A pending decision is not a playable rule — and neither is a settled one that was never written into the document that owns it. §2 is currently empty: every decision this log tracks is written in.

## 1. Settled boundaries

- VBG 2.2 uses `1d20 + Stat + applicable Trait bonus` and TNs 10 / 15 / 20 / 25+.
- Results use Failure, Success with Cost, Clean Success, and Critical Success by the margins in the Player Rulebook.
- Character creation starts with 4 Strain, no Armor, 4 slots, one Major Trait, one Minor Trait, one Ability Domain, and up to two allowed items.
- Abilities use 0 / 1 / 2 Strain Minor / Standard / Major scales. Ability scale and action type are distinct.
- Threat scenes use one Major Action, one Minor Action, one conditional response, Free Actions, and a 24-hour cycle. There is no initiative and no turn order.
- **Every hit deals exactly 1 damage.** Nothing raises it: not ability scale, not a Critical Success, not a weakness, and no item. Weapons grant effects, never damage.
- All damage is physical unless the rule dealing it says otherwise.
- A cycle resolves as movement → actions and threats together → conditional responses → consequences. All hits land, excess damage is wasted, and a character Downed in a cycle still resolves their declared action.
- A threat is addressed by the Major Action, answered by a conditional response, or it applies. There is no defensive roll.
- Being Downed inflicts an Injury and starts a deadline. Death requires a stated killing or an announced lethal danger, and nothing else.
- Scenes have a perception layer: **engagement**, **cover**, **awareness** (Unaware / Searching / Aware), **light** (Lit / Dim / Dark), and a 0–6 **Noise Clock** per area. Creatures never roll, so cover buys action economy rather than difficulty.
- Creatures do not roll and use Resistance as their only defeat track.
- Progression limits are Stat +5, maximum Strain 10, inventory 6 slots, three Domains, and uncapped trait ownership. Only one relevant Major and one relevant Minor apply to a roll.
- PP is awarded per session or arc, 2–5 typically, equally to every player. It buys the six listed upgrades and is never currency.
- Character creation spends all 6 points, no Stat below −1 and none above +2.
- Only threat scenes use the cycle. Investigation, social play, downtime, and travel are freeform, and long travel uses a Transition Scene.
- Items have no damage values. Weapons grant effects; acquisition runs on four paths and three value bands, with no currency.

## 2b. Decisions settled 2026-09-24 — the perception layer

A second audit found that 2.2 had **no mechanics for sound, sight, detection, cover, or engagement at all**, despite the rules referring to all five. Stealth was one word in a TN table. The missed-post default — "their character takes cover" — pointed at a term the ruleset never defined. Meanwhile the sibling VBG-Z module had shipped a working 0–6 Noise Clock engine that 2.2 dropped on the split.

This was the load-bearing gap: the ruleset repeatedly told players to take cover, move unseen, and avoid notice, and defined none of it.

| Gap | Decision | Where it now lives |
|---|---|---|
| **No definition of "engaged"**, though Escape, movement, and threat rules all used it | Engaged means something within Melee / Reach actively pressing you. Accompanying movement cannot leave the band while engaged. | Player Rulebook §4 |
| **No cover rules**, though the missed-post default is to take cover | Three states. Because creatures never roll, cover buys **action economy**: partial cover answers one ranged threat per cycle with a Free Action; full cover blocks ranged threats in both directions. | Player Rulebook §4 |
| **No awareness or detection** | Three states per creature, per character: Unaware, Searching, Aware. Stated openly in the scene post. Acting against an Unaware target resolves **one outcome band better** — never more damage, since nothing deals more than 1. | Player Rulebook §4 |
| **No light or visibility** | Lit / Dim / Dark. Dim pushes sight-dependent targets one band further; Dark removes sight past Melee both ways. A light source fixes it and announces you. | Player Rulebook §4 |
| **No noise mechanics** | Every action is Silent / Quiet (+1) / Loud (+2). Each area runs a **0–6 Noise Clock**, stated in every scene post, never hidden. 4–5 brings a search and +2 TN to move unseen; 6 brings arrival and resets to 1. Decays −1 per quiet cycle or on leaving the area. | Player Rulebook §4 |
| **No stealth procedure** | Moving unseen is an ordinary declared action at TN 10 / 15 / 20 by conditions. Failure ticks the clock and raises awareness. Dark or full cover skips the roll against sight-based creatures. | Player Rulebook §4 |
| **No pursuit procedure**, though Escape and movement referenced it | A pursuer closes one band per cycle unless held off. Two consecutive cycles at greater distance loses it. | Player Rulebook §4 |
| **No assist rule**, though helping was referenced | Spend a Major Action to give another character's roll +1. One assist per roll, no stacking. | Player Rulebook §4 |
| **Creature cards could not answer "does the dark help?"** | **Senses** is now a required card field, with what the creature cannot perceive stated. Cards also carry the noise they make and their starting awareness. | Creature Blueprint §3 |
| **Items had no noise** | Every item entry carries a noise rating. Firearms are Loud; blades and bows are Silent; a suppressed weapon and a hooded lamp are the Rare answers. | Item Glossary §1, §3, §6 |
| **One clock per module** | The track, milestones, and decay belong to the Player Rulebook. A module may add **triggers** only — VBG-Z by calibre, for instance — so a scene runs one clock, not two. | Player Rulebook §4 |

> **Historical setting exception retired, 2026-09-27.** The removed setting's noise exception does not apply to core VBG. The Player Rulebook §4 owns Noise Clock triggers, including ability use; this decision record does not override it.

## 2c. Decisions settled 2026-09-24 — the carry-through pass

A third audit found that **§2a and §2b recorded rulings that were never written into the documents they named**. The Player Rulebook was still at its 2026-09-20 text: no perception layer, no cycle order, no conditional-response rule, no Injury procedure — and a §6 line stating that a Downed character *"dies if struck again"*, the exact opposite of D1. The Item Glossary was still a checklist of empty boxes while D2 claimed it was written in full.

A recorded decision that is not in the rules text is not a rule. This pass wrote all of them in, and settled the last five open items so that nothing in VBG 2.2 is pending.

| Item | Decision | Where it now lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Carry-through** | Every §2a and §2b ruling is now written into the Player Rulebook: key terms, the veto standard, cost and critical limits, the cycle order, conditional responses, threat answering, engagement, Escape TNs, pursuit, cover, awareness, light, the Noise Clock, moving unseen, assist, sustained-effect limits, hazard overlap, damage as physical, Downed and Injury, death conditions, Strain Transfer to a Downed character, and the corrected worked example at 4 → 2. | Player Rulebook §1–§9 |
| **D2 completion** | The Item Glossary is written as a rules text rather than a checklist: entry format with a required noise rating, four acquisition paths, three value bands, weapons as effects with no damage values, ammunition by stack, healing and treatment with a one-consumable-per-scene limit, three Armor grades, tools and kits with defined contents, material units, scrap, and crafting. | Item Glossary §1–§9 |
| **D3** | **Settled.** PP is awarded per session or completed arc, equally to every player, never per cycle and never for damage dealt. +1 for surviving a real risk, avoiding violence, a discovery, or costly roleplay; +2 for a completed objective; +3 for a campaign milestone. A typical session is 2–5 PP. The only additional prerequisite is a fictional reason that appeared in play. PP is not currency. | Player Rulebook §8, GM Guide §8 |
| **D5a** | **Settled.** A Transition Scene runs as one to three legs, each with one obstacle and one roll — individually or one roll for the group with assists. Success with Cost spends a real resource; Failure delays the group, misplaces them, or converts the leg into an ordinary threat scene. Arriving without pursuit starts the destination one clock lower. No PP is awarded for travel itself. | Player Rulebook §4, GM Guide §5 |
| **Q13 placement** | Domain approval is now an owned section with a four-test standard and six worked verdicts, plus the instruction to counter-offer rather than simply reject. GM Guide sections renumbered accordingly. | GM Guide §3 |
| **Q14** | **Settled.** VBG 2.2 ships **no trait catalog** and does not adopt the v1.4.2 catalogs. Traits are fiction-first and a catalog is not needed to play. A setting module may supply its own. | Player Rulebook §3 |
| **Q15** | **Settled.** Non-threat play is **deliberately freeform**: investigation, social play, and downtime use §2 resolution and nothing else — no cycle, no action economy, no post order. The perception layer still runs underneath it. Long travel is the one exception and uses a Transition Scene. | Player Rulebook §4 |
| **Q16** | **Settled.** All 6 points are spent, no Stat may be reduced below −1 to buy points elsewhere, and +2 / +2 / −1 is legal and intended. | Player Rulebook §3 |
| **Q29 wording** | The GM Guide no longer says "vulnerabilities". Every document in the set calls the card field **Weaknesses**. | Creature Blueprint §3.3, GM Guide §7 |

## 3. Editorial decisions

- **E1 — Rule authority:** shared player-facing mechanics live in the Player Rulebook; the GM Guide supplies procedures; creature construction and item definitions have separate owners. The [index](README.md) §3 states the full authority table.
- **E2 — Status language:** pending rules are recorded here and carry a short linked notice at the affected rule, so a reader knows the procedure is unavailable without leaving the page.
- **E3 — Historical documents:** `archive/revision-notes/refinement-backlog.md` and `docs/baseline-comparison.md` are historical working notes covering base v1.4.2 and VBG-Z. They retain their recorded scopes and do not override the current core. The comparison also records Arcana Deck proposals separately from historical observations.
- **E4 — Presentation:** all five documents plus the index share one status block, numbered sections, and one navigation footer, so any rule can be cited as *document § number*. Terminology is defined once in Player Rulebook §1 and used without redefinition elsewhere.
