# Living World — Player Roleplay Module Plan

> **Archived 2026-09-27 — historical material, not current rules.** Setting references describe a removed setting. Use the repository’s `rules/` and `modules/` indexes for current material.

**Status:** proposed design, not approved rules · **Revised:** 2026-09-24  
**Owns:** proposed procedures for player-created social scenes, relationships, commitments, and roleplay evidence.

**Confirmed brief:** VBG 2.2; Port Vane; 20–40 active players; mostly asynchronous play; opt-in PvP with agreed stakes and staff handling lasting consequences.
**Dependencies:** Campaign Format (removed setting; historical reference) owns setting authority; [World Systems Plan](world-systems-plan.md) owns projects and awards; [Discord Operations Plan](discord-operations-plan.md) owns server time, concurrency, and review cadence.
**Boundary:** This plan supplies a candidate procedure for non-threat play, currently unresolved as **Q15**. It does not make pending core rules playable.
**Decision references:** **D** IDs: [core Decision Log](../../rules/decision-log.md); **Q** IDs: [candidate review questions](../revision-notes/open-questions.md); **P** IDs: Port Vane Open Questions (removed setting; historical reference).

## 1. Make ordinary life carry the campaign

Players need repeatable reasons to approach, trust, disappoint, and depend on one another without waiting for a contract or a GM event.

| Scene invitation | Decision that gives it weight | Next scene it can create |
|---|---|---|
| Two Strays discuss who gets protected during an evacuation. | Someone accepts a responsibility and someone refuses it. | Recruitment, reconciliation, or a request to the Marshals. |
| Contract and Salvage members dispute a previous allocation. | They agree what each will ask of their superiors. | A DM resolves the competing requests. |
| A frightened new Domain user asks a veteran for advice. | The veteran shares an approved fact or admits uncertainty. | Mentoring and a planned training scene. |
| A crew confronts a member over a broken promise. | They choose whether and on what terms to continue working together. | A revised compact or a consensual rivalry. |
| Residents plan a gathering after an incursion. | PCs choose its purpose, invitations, and contributions. | The gathering itself; public effects await review. |

Quiet conversation, humour, friendship, grief, and ordinary companionship remain valid play without a deliverable or reward claim. The procedure below is available when a decision, continuity issue, or contested stake needs clarity.

## 2. Preserve the authority split

In ordinary social scenes, **players decide what their characters do; a DM decides what the world does back.** A social host gains no additional fictional authority. [Encounter Running](encounter-running-plan.md) adds a separate, scoped exception for authorised players resolving approved monster fights, including endorsed play-and-run participation. It does not let an ordinary social host invent NPC reactions or rewards.

| Participants can establish and log | Stop at a proposal; a DM decides |
|---|---|
| Their PCs' speech, intentions, emotions, and freely chosen responses. | NPC responses, faction orders, investigations, recruitment, and standing. |
| A voluntary friendship, personal promise, grudge, or working arrangement. | Licence, rank, property rights, official appointment, or institutional debt. |
| Repeating information their PCs already know, with its source. | New discoveries, verified rumours, and hidden setting facts. |
| Planning to obtain, lend, give, or exchange something. | Gear gained or lost, ownership transfer, item creation, and mechanical resources. |
| Positioning and ordinary time within the established situation. | Declaring a dangerous route safe, advancing a threat, or awarding recovery. |
| Consented personal conflict within the agreed scope. | Character death, permanent injury, or any consequence imposed without consent. |

- Minor resource use follows Campaign Format §2; it cannot turn undefined consumables into free inventory or bypass **D2**.
- An agreed personal conclusion can support subsequent conversation; its log still enters batch review for continuity. It grants no provisional gear, standing, discovery, or other world change.
- A disputed or authority-crossing consequence stays unresolved until review. Continue unrelated dialogue; do not act on the disputed claim elsewhere.
- Descriptive colour must stay within published facts. Players cannot introduce a helpful shopkeeper, unlocked clinic, new safehouse, or Carrion sighting as scenery.
- Player organisations are crews and associations inside the existing city. A charter does not replace Port Vane's factions or override the two-membership limit.

## 3. Proposed social scene procedure

Use one Discord thread per contained situation; the opening card can be five short lines. A casual scene can acquire a card when its stakes grow.

1. **Invite.** Give a place, in-fiction time, purpose, participation limit, and posting pace. State whether the scene is open, invitation-only, or seeking a particular role.
2. **Set boundaries.** Each participant accepts the premise and any conflict stakes. Name subjects to omit or fade out; no explanation is required. Consent to a premise is not consent to every escalation.
3. **State wants.** Each PC names an immediate objective through play or a short out-of-character note: an apology, a partner, a confidence, an agreement. Private motives can stay private.
4. **Play a beat.** Offer, ask, reveal, challenge, concede, or refuse. Give addressed participants an opportunity to respond; a beat is a useful conversational unit, not a combat cycle or action budget.
5. **Land a choice.** The affected player chooses their PC's response. Record an agreement, changed relationship, clear refusal, or unresolved issue; a scene need not force consensus.
6. **Close and log.** Every participant can correct the summary. Separate personal conclusions, unverified claims, and requested world consequences. Submit once for the whole scene.

For a difficult negotiation, make the terms explicit: **request → reason → offer → reservation → revised offer or refusal**. Each side can identify what they want to preserve. This creates decisions without a social hit-point track.

Cooperation needs no manufactured obstacle: PCs may agree immediately, divide responsibilities, rehearse, or help one another. Training does not automatically grant Traits, PP, or improved equipment.

## 4. Persuasion, secrets, and consensual conflict

### Social agency

- A persuasive speech can influence another player; no self-run roll dictates a PC's beliefs, feelings, loyalty, romance, confession, or forgiveness.
- A player may voluntarily choose an unwanted in-character outcome because it makes good drama. They choose the response and its scope.
- A PC can lie about their own actions or intentions. A player's invented world claim is a rumour or allegation, not a discovery. Out-of-character logs identify that distinction.
- Reading a public thread does not give a PC its contents. An information entry needs a source and a fictional delivery route; secrecy against characters does not require secrecy against moderators.
- Perception, mind-reading, truth detection, coercive Domains, and exposure of protected secrets require approved scope and adjudication. They are not shortcuts around consent or **P1**.

### Conflict gate

Before theft, threats meant to constrain another PC, sabotage, capture, a duel, or betrayal with material effects, establish:

| Agreement | Required content |
|---|---|
| Participants | Every directly affected player opts in; faction membership is not blanket consent. |
| Objective | What each side attempts and what a win can actually change. |
| Stakes | Allowed losses and prohibited outcomes; separate personal stakes from staff-owned consequences. |
| Resolution | A mutually written outcome or a staff-run procedure that has been approved for this module. |
| Exit | How to pause, revise, fade out, or abandon escalation without a default in-fiction defeat. |

Consent is necessary but does not grant GM authority. An agreed gear theft still needs a DM; a consensual duel still needs approved PvP combat procedures; lasting harm always goes to staff.

If a player declines, revise toward conversation, mutually agreed rivalry, or another scene. Do not force the refused consequence by involving an NPC, a faction, an alternate character, or a third player. Staff can pause or unwind disputed content rather than awarding victory.

**Recommended first release:** resolve self-run social choices through agreement; route unresolved mechanical contests to a DM. VBG's player-versus-creature TN procedure is not an approved opposed-PC system. A later bounded social-roll option needs explicit design and consent rules under **Q15/D4**; no such option is established here.

## 5. Relationships, promises, and personal debts

Keep these short and fictional; they create invitations and decisions rather than bonuses.

| Record | Fields | Authority and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Relationship | Other PC; what I value or distrust; unresolved question; last relevant scene. | Each player owns their view. Mutual labels need both players' agreement. |
| Promise | Who promises what to whom; trigger or deadline; scope; acknowledgement; fulfilled, revised, broken, or released. | Never compels a future player action. The recipient chooses their personal response to a breach. |
| Personal favour | Specific voluntary undertaking; creditor; debtor; why it matters; settlement scene. | Not currency, transferable leverage, PP, or automatic control over a character. |
| Crew compact | Purpose; responsibilities; joining/leaving process; dispute process; current participants. | No faction standing, exclusive territory, equipment pool, or rank granted by the document itself. |

Institutional Ledger debt belongs to the world economy. A PC cannot erase it by calling it a favour, promise an NPC's resources, or collect a mechanical penalty from another player.

Suggested starting prompts: one person you want to impress; one boundary you refuse to cross; one offer you can make now; one favour you might ask for. These are optional invitations, not mandatory backstory debts.

Revisit records when a scene changes them. Avoid mandatory weekly relationship upkeep or penalties for an absent friend.

## 6. Let ambitions generate play

A player ambition should name a desired change, why this PC cares, a first social action, and what would count as evidence. Players can revise or abandon it without forfeiting participation credit.

| Ambition | Self-run first action | World boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Start a neighbourhood mutual-aid circle. | Recruit consenting PCs and agree who will ask for supplies. | Supplies, premises, NPC participation, and district benefit need ratification. |
| Reconcile a Contract/Salvage crew split. | Let PCs set terms for working together again. | Division policy, salvage ownership, and employer reactions remain DM-owned. |
| Challenge a Ledger practice. | Assemble testimony already established in play; agree who speaks publicly. | New evidence, public reach, retaliation, and policy change need a DM. |
| Establish a memorial or support gathering. | Invite PCs; share memories; choose a programme. | Permits, crowds, money raised, and changed public sentiment need review. |
| Learn how another PC handles their Domain. | Ask, observe an already approved harmless use, discuss technique. | Domain expansion, safety claims, mechanical training, and loudness consequences need adjudication. |

Use the [World Systems Plan](world-systems-plan.md) for a project spanning multiple scenes. This document supplies contribution evidence, not a second project clock or progress award.

Player events can proceed with consenting PCs in an established suitable place. A host submits only exceptional needs to the DM queue. An event has no minimum turnout and gains no automatic status from being public.

Keep an invitation feed for **offers, needs, meetings, and open relationships**, not only missions. Staff can seed a social dilemma through a published situation; players decide whom to approach and what to do about it.

## 7. Asynchronous play without social hostage-taking

Global time, simultaneous-scene limits, absence defaults, and closure deadlines belong to [Discord Operations Plan](discord-operations-plan.md). The social interface should apply them as follows:

- An opening card states response pace and closure date. Speed is never an initiative advantage, proof of consent, or a claim to extra authority.
- Addressed players get the agreed response window before the next irreversible conversational beat. Others can post atmosphere or their own reactions without resolving the absent PC's choice.
- A participant can write a short standing instruction: “If I miss the next beat, I leave politely; I make no agreement.” It authorizes only the stated personal response.
- Without an instruction, record the missing response as unresolved. Others may conclude their own decisions; nobody authors the absent PC's assent, insult, betrayal, injury, or surrender.
- Close a stalled scene with agreed facts and outstanding questions. Closing a Discord thread does not settle those questions, advance a recovery timer, or create Catch Your Breath eligibility.
- Backdated conversation uses knowledge available at its recorded time. It cannot grant an earlier item, retroactively cancel a danger, or import a later discovery.
- Keep two types of change visible: **personal conclusion** and **pending world request**. A pending request cannot finance or justify a second scene's material outcome.
- When a scene becomes threatening, hand resolution to an authorised encounter runner if an approved packet covers it; otherwise request a DM. Freeze only unsupported escalation. PvP still follows its separate consent/staff procedure, and compatible dialogue elsewhere may continue.

## 8. Recognise meaningful play without farming it

Mechanical progression and award amounts belong to the [World Systems Plan](world-systems-plan.md) and unresolved core **D3**. This module should provide evidence, not pay by message, word count, thread, dice roll, attendance streak, or reciprocal endorsement.

Useful evidence is a consequential choice, a changed relationship, a commitment tested, help that enabled another PC's decision, or a contribution to an established project. Failure, refusal, compromise, support roles, and brief posts can all qualify.

- Use one contribution summary per player per review period, citing a representative scene; every participant need not submit the same transcript.
- Repeating the same staged dispute, recycling a promise between friends, or splitting one conversation into many threads does not multiply evidence.
- Review describes what changed and whose decision mattered. It does not rank prose quality, dramatic suffering, popularity, or how many partners a player has.
- Existing friendships are welcome; staff look for duplicated claims or fabricated changes, not a minimum number of different partners.
- Scene invitations and useful introductions are visible, nonmechanical forms of recognition. They carry no exclusive access or standing.

## 9. Reusable Discord templates

Keep the closure summary around 100–150 words; omit empty fields. Link the thread so a DM can inspect disputed details without reading every ordinary conversation in full.

### Opening card

```text
SCENE: [title] | HOST: [player; coordinator only]
WHERE / WHEN: [approved place; canonical time] | SOURCE: [current world notice if relevant]
INVITATION: [open / invited PCs; purpose; participant limit]
PACE / CLOSE: [response expectation; proposed closure]
BOUNDARIES: [tone; excluded content; conflict allowed only after explicit agreement]
WANTS / STAKES: [what can change personally; any world request stays pending]
```

### Scene log

```text
SCENE LINK / TIME / PARTICIPANTS:
PERSONAL CONCLUSIONS: [choices, promises, relationship changes; participant acknowledgement]
CLAIMS MADE: [known source / PC belief / allegation; no new world facts implied]
WORLD REQUESTS: [one precise requested consequence per entry, or none]
RESOURCES / INJURIES: [existing records and proposed changes; no self-awarded gains]
OPEN OR DISPUTED: [questions; absent participants; pending consent]
CONTRIBUTION EVIDENCE: [one sentence about a meaningful choice, if submitted for review]
REVIEW: [pending / reviewed; DM; approved changes; correction with reason]
```

### Commitment / ambition card

```text
PEOPLE / DESIRED CHANGE:
PROMISE OR FIRST STEP: [specific action; not a guaranteed world result]
TRIGGER / REVIEW DATE:
EVIDENCE LINKS:
STATUS: [offered / agreed / fulfilled / revised / broken / released]
DM-OWNED OUTCOME REQUESTED: [or none]
```

## 10. Complete example: a promise at the Black Ditch

**Illustrative setup, not new canon:** a DM has published notice `W-17`: yesterday's bridge evacuation left witnesses seeking someone to hear their accounts. No official inquiry or available compensation has been established.

**Opening.** Two Strays, Nera and Oren, invite a third, Suri, to an established meeting place in the Flats. Their card cites `W-17`, gives the canonical evening, sets the server's social posting pace, and limits stakes to sharing accounts and making personal commitments. No combat or equipment exchanges are proposed.

**First beat.** Nera asks Oren to accompany her when she requests an audience with the Marshals. Oren says he distrusts them and asks why she needs him. Suri volunteers to listen but will not sign an account she did not witness. Each player writes only their own PC's response.

**Second beat.** Nera reveals she did not personally see the bridge rise; her earlier accusation was an assumption. She withdraws her request for Oren to endorse that claim and asks him only to describe what he saw. Oren accepts on condition that Nera keeps his testimony separate from her allegation. Suri offers to help them practise the request.

**Decision.** Nera promises that separation. Oren promises to accompany her if an audience is arranged. Suri agrees to help rehearse but declines further involvement. No persuasion roll is needed: each player freely chooses the response. Oren's player records “I trust Nera's honesty more; I still doubt her judgment.”

**Closure log.** All three acknowledge the promises and changed relationship. The log marks the original accusation as an unverified PC belief. It requests one world response: whether a Marshal will hear Nera and Oren. It records no gear, standing, PP, discovery, injury, or successful investigation. Contribution evidence is Nera's choice to correct her own account and Oren's conditional commitment.

**Batch review.** The DM confirms consistency of the personal scene and leaves the audience request queued for world adjudication. Players can discuss their preparations immediately; they cannot tell others that an inquiry has opened. A later DM response might offer an appointment, require another contact, or refuse the request. None is predetermined by the successful social scene.

## 11. Drafting and playtest gates

| Candidate extension | Dependency | What to establish before approval |
|---|---|---|
| Non-threat scene and closure procedure. | **Q15**, timing **D5/Q12**. | Social beats are not combat cycles; thread closure cannot mint recovery or advance threats. |
| Any later social-roll option. | **Q15**, consequence limits **D4**, Trait relevance **Q7**. | Exact trigger, authority, all outcome boundaries, and maintained player control; no opposed-roll rule assumed. |
| RP contribution evidence for progression. | **D3**. | Same review standard for quiet and prolific players; no per-scene award. |
| Social transfers and shared crew resources. | **D2/Q4**, Port Vane **P5**. | Acquisition, ownership, inventory, and review procedure; no free item creation. |
| Faction commitments and divided loyalty. | Port Vane **P2/P3/P4/P7**. | Personal agreement never grants rank or decides competing institutional demands. |
| Ability-assisted social play. | Port Vane **P1**, scope guidance **Q13**. | Approved effects, consent boundaries, costs, and DM-owned loudness consequences. |

Pilot with several friendship, negotiation, rivalry, and project-planning scenes, including a missed post and a contested log. Check that players can finish personal scenes without a live DM, unresolved requests do not propagate as facts, two friends cannot multiply award evidence, and refusal or absence never becomes automatic defeat.
