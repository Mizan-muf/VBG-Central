# Port Vane — Domains

**Status:** working draft · **Revised:** 2026-09-24
**Owns:** the Domain catalog, loudness, Dead Static, Burnout, and setting gear.

> Mechanics come from [VBG 2.2](../vbg-2.2/README.md). This document supplies a catalog, a noise trigger, one added track, and gear entries. Where it looks like it is changing a rule, read the rule.

## 1. What a Domain is here

A Domain is an **Ability Domain** in VBG's sense — a GM-approved sphere of effect, produced by spending Strain, at 0 / 1 / 2 Strain for Minor / Standard / Major scale ([Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §5). Everything the rulebook says about scale, action cost, reach, sustaining, and hazards applies unchanged.

What Port Vane adds is what a Domain **costs you socially**. In this city a Domain is not a gift. It is:

- **A licence or a crime.** The Iron Ledger sells the Sanction; without one, using a Domain in public is a chargeable offence and a bounty listing.
- **A debt.** Use ruins the body, and the drug that repairs the body is sold on credit at interest.
- **A beacon.** Carrion are addicted to Stigma. Using your Domain solves the problem in front of you and calls a worse one.

### Choosing one

Pick one entry from §3, or write your own to the same shape and have the DM approve it against [GM Guide](../vbg-2.2/GM%20Guide.md) §3. Every Domain in this catalog states three things, and yours must too:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Scope** | One sentence naming the medium and its limit. It is what the DM approved and what arguments are settled against. |
| **Loudness** | Quiet, Moderate, or Loud — the noise trigger in §2. This is the primary axis separating one Domain from another here. |
| **Signature cost** | The Success-with-Cost this Domain tends to pay. A suggestion, not a rule: the player still proposes the cost ([Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §2). |

**Nothing in this catalog deals more than 1 damage,** because nothing in VBG does. A Loud Domain is not stronger than a Quiet one. It reaches further, breaks more, and costs you the room.

A character may hold up to three Domains, bought with PP at 20 each. Most people in Port Vane have one and wish they had none.

## 2. Loudness, the Noise Clock, and Dead Static

Domain loudness is **a noise trigger feeding VBG's shared Noise Clock**, not a second track. One area, one clock, 0–6, stated in every scene post ([Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §4).

| Port Vane loudness | VBG noise rating | Ticks |
|---|---|---:|
| **Quiet** | Silent | 0 |
| **Moderate** | Quiet | +1 |
| **Loud** | Loud | +2 |

Two consequences the table should sit with.

**Gunfire fills the clock as fast as a Domain flare.** A shotgun is Loud. A kinetic burst is Loud. They cost the area exactly the same. That makes the Ledger's Contract crews the noisiest thing in any district they enter, and it means **the Rupture Cult is only partly right**: Stigma draws the Carrion, but so does a precinct's worth of buckshot, and the Cult's theology never accounts for that.

**The quiet Domains are not the weak ones.** Residue, Hush, Knit, and Rot look unimpressive on a card. In a district where the clock reaching 6 means something comes through a wall, they are how crews survive long enough to get paid.

### Dead Static

Dead Static is the electromagnetic deadening that surrounds Carrion activity. It is no longer only colour:

> **Dead Static applies while an area's Noise Clock stands at 4 or higher.** Radios, phones, dashcams, electric light, and anything else that needs current stop working for as long as the clock stays there.

This is mechanical, and it bites the factions unevenly.

- **Marshals** lose dispatch, which is how coordinated evacuation fails.
- **Contract crews** lose the bounty ticker and the ability to call for a second crew.
- **Salvage** loses the trucks' radios, and arrives at a cordon they cannot see into.
- **A character with Static Ear or Carry** becomes the only communication the group has, which is a reason to bring one and a reason someone would take one from you.

Two places carry the condition permanently, both already in the [Setting Guide](Setting%20Guide.md): a **static corridor** opens its scene with the clock already at 4, and a **Cairn-Titan** holds the clock at 4 or higher for as long as it is present ([Carrion](Carrion.md) §2).

## 3. The catalog

Twenty-four Domains, four in each of six niches. Written for this city: every one of them has a reason to exist in a place where the corpses are a commodity and the phones do not work.

### Force

Direct, destructive, and almost always the wrong choice in a district you want to leave quietly.

| Domain | Scope | Loudness | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kinetic** | Shoves, crushes, and redirects the momentum of things I can see. | **Loud** | Something behind the target breaks too, and it belonged to someone. |
| **Flare** | Kindles and shapes open fire, whether it already burns or I start it by hand. | **Loud** | The fire outlives the ability and goes somewhere you did not choose. |
| **Arc** | Draws current from any live or stored source and throws it. | **Loud** | The source is spent — the lights, the truck battery, the thing you were going to need. |
| **Sunder** | Weakens the structural integrity of worked material I touch. | Moderate | The weakening spreads: the floor, the stair, the wall you are standing against. |

### Control

The ground, the doors, and what is in the way. The workhorses of an incursion.

| Domain | Scope | Loudness | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ballast** | Makes objects within sight heavier or lighter than they are. | Moderate | It reverts at the worst moment, and whatever you lightened comes down. |
| **Tide** | Moves standing water and whatever is floating in it. | Moderate | What the water carries goes where the water goes, including the contamination. |
| **Bind** | Makes cable, rope, chain, and wire move and grip on command. | Quiet | The line takes hold of the wrong thing, and letting go takes a cycle. |
| **Threshold** | Seals or unseals a doorway, gate, or gap for as long as I hold it. | Moderate | It seals both ways, and somebody is on the wrong side. |

### Perception

Quiet, informational, and the reason a crew comes home. In a city with Dead Static, knowing things is a supply problem.

| Domain | Scope | Loudness | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Residue** | Reads what recently happened in a place by touching its surfaces. | **Quiet** | You get the moment, not the context, and it is somebody's worst one. |
| **Tell** | Reads physiological stress in a person I can see: heart, breath, sweat, tremor. | **Quiet** | They notice you looking, and they are right about what it means. |
| **Bloom** | Extends awareness through an enclosed space as a felt map of what is in it. | **Quiet** | You feel everything in the space, including what is inside the walls. |
| **Static Ear** | Hears through Dead Static and pulls signal out of interference. | **Quiet** | You hear something on the band that nobody transmitted. |

### Body

Yours, or someone else's. Three of these are what a crew's medic actually is.

| Domain | Scope | Loudness | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Knit** | Closes wounds on a person I touch. *(A Strain Transfer ability — [Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §6 owns the procedure.)* | **Quiet** | It closes wrong, and the Injury is now yours to explain. |
| **Anchor** | Makes my own body immovable where it stands. | **Quiet** | Immovable includes not being able to leave. |
| **Stride** | Moves my own body faster and further than a body should go. | Moderate | You arrive before your footing does. |
| **Hollow** | Takes harm meant for someone within reach onto myself instead. | **Quiet** | You take it, and you take it whole — including the Injury. |

### Voice

Sound, speech, and crowds. Underrated until the radios die.

| Domain | Scope | Loudness | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hush** | Dampens sound in a space I can see. | **Quiet** | Dampened for everyone: no warning shouts, no listening for what is coming. |
| **Carry** | Sends my voice to one person I have met, anywhere in the district. | **Quiet** | It carries both ways for a moment, and they hear where you are. |
| **Press** | Puts unbearable weight behind a single spoken command. | Moderate | It works, and they remember exactly what was done to them. |
| **Chorus** | Makes a crowd feel what I feel. | **Loud** | A crowd is a poor instrument. They keep feeling it after you stop. |

### Anti-Carrion

Domains that do something specific to the things that come through tears. Rare, valuable, and the reason Salvage will hire someone with no other qualification.

| Domain | Scope | Loudness | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rot** | Accelerates decay in dead organic matter I touch. | **Quiet** | The corpse is worth less. Salvage noticed. |
| **Solvent** | Dissolves the bonds holding grafted matter together. | Moderate | It does not distinguish grafted from original, on anyone. |
| **Rust** | Corrodes metal, including the inorganic grafts in Carrion plate. | Moderate | Everything metal nearby corrodes — your weapon, the catwalk, the cuffs. |
| **Quarantine** | Holds a Rupture, or a wound in a Carrion, shut for as long as I hold it. | **Loud** | Holding it is sustained, and letting go is worse than never starting. |

**How the anti-Carrion four actually work.** They do not add damage. They satisfy **creature conditions** ([Creature Blueprint](../vbg-2.2/Creature%20Blueprint.md) §3.3): Solvent and Rust crack a Carapace-Render's plate so that ordinary attacks can remove boxes at all, Rot ends a Shiverer's cohesion without a fight and ruins the salvage, and Quarantine is how a Cairn stops opening secondary tears while the demolition crew works. That is why a Salvage Factor will pay for a Rust user who cannot fight: the whole engagement changes length.

## 4. Burnout

Burnout is Port Vane's one addition to a character sheet: a **0–4 track**, alongside Strain. Strain is what you spend. Burnout is what spending it does to you.

### Taking it

| Trigger | Burnout |
|---|---|
| Going **Downed by your own Strain expenditure** — spending your last Strain on an ability rather than being hit down to 0. | +1 |
| **Overreach:** paying an ability's Strain cost you cannot afford. Take 1 Burnout for each Strain you are short. Resolve the ability, then go Downed. | +1 per Strain short |

Damage never causes Burnout. Being beaten unconscious is ordinary harm; burning yourself out is a choice you made.

Overreach is the one thing this module adds to the ability rules, and it is deliberate: it is the mechanical shape of the Cult's entire argument. You *can* always do the thing. It just costs the part of you that would still be here next month.

### Carrying it

| Burnout | Effect |
|---:|---|
| **0** | Clean. Registered users test at this level and are believed. |
| **1** | It shows — nosebleeds, ash taste, a tremor. No mechanical effect, and everyone who matters can read it on you. |
| **2** | **Catch Your Breath no longer works for you.** Strain comes back only from rest, medicine, or another person's Domain. |
| **3** | **Every Domain you use rises one loudness step.** Quiet becomes Moderate, Moderate becomes Loud, and Loud stays Loud while getting considerably worse to be near. |
| **4** | **The threshold.** The maximum; there is no 5. Grey-market doses no longer clear it (§5). |

Burnout 3 is where the campaign's economy becomes personal. A Residue reader at Burnout 3 is ticking the clock every time they touch a wall, which means they need stabilisers, which means they need a contract, which means they burn out further. That spiral is the Iron Ledger's actual product.

### Clearing it

**One stabiliser dose clears 1 Burnout, in downtime, and nothing else does.** Not rest, not medicine, not a Domain, not time. At Burnout 4, only pharmaceutical-grade doses work.

### The threshold

At Burnout 4 the character stands at the crossing point the Cult calls ascension and the Ledger calls a containment event.

> **The player alone decides whether to cross.** Not the DM, not a roll, not a clock, not another player. There is no trigger that forces it and no accumulating count that reaches it on its own.

**Burnout never kills.** A character can sit at 4 for an entire campaign, one dose from the edge, and that is a perfectly good character to play. Crossing is an ending a player may choose to write — the character leaves as something else, and what they become belongs to the DM and the setting afterward. It is a retirement, not a death, and it cannot be done to someone.

## 5. Setting gear

Written to the [Item Glossary](../vbg-2.2/Item%20Glossary.md) format and bands. Everything the base glossary contains is available in Port Vane; these are the entries the setting adds.

### Stabilisers and medicine

| Item | Slot | Band | Noise | Effect | Uses |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **Stabiliser dose, pharmaceutical** | 1/4 | Restricted | Silent | Over downtime, clears **1 Burnout**. The only thing that clears Burnout 4. This is what the debt is for. | Single use |
| **Grey stabiliser** | 1/4 | Common | Silent | Over downtime, clears **1 Burnout**. Refined from Shiverer sludge. **A dose taken at Burnout 3 or 4 also inflicts one Condition the DM names**, and does not clear the Burnout. | Single use |
| **Field ampoule** | 1/4 | Restricted | Silent | Free Action: restore **1 Strain**. Counts as the scene's one healing consumable ([Item Glossary](../vbg-2.2/Item%20Glossary.md) §4), and the next scene begins with 1 less maximum Strain. | Single use |

**The pipeline in one line:** a month of pharmaceutical doses is a **Standing** bounty ([Carrion](Carrion.md) §4), a character at Burnout 3 needs three of them, and a Ledger balance is what covers the difference.

### Salvage and munitions

| Item | Slot | Band | Noise | Effect | Uses |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **Salvage kit** | 1 | Restricted | Silent | Required to recover a Carrion corpse's value. **Without one, a bounty pays one band lower**, and a Tier I corpse pays nothing at all. Contents: bone saw, gland flasks, cut-resistant gauntlets, cold pack. | 4 corpses |
| **Anti-anomaly munitions** | 1/4 | Restricted | **Loud** | Refined from Carrion tissue. Counts as a **breaching tool** against a Carrion creature condition at any band a firearm reaches — the standard answer to a Carapace-Render's plate. Requires a firearm. | Stack of 6 |
| **Scent-mask, diesel and sulfur** | 1/4 | Common | Silent | Carrion that hunt by smell — Shiverers above all — treat the wearer as **one awareness step lower**. Does nothing against sight, sound, or Stigma. | One scene |
| **Dampening cuffs** | 1/2 | **Rare** | Silent | A restrained person cannot use a Domain. Ledger Cellar equipment; possessing a pair outside the Ledger is itself a charge. | — |

### City equipment

| Item | Slot | Band | Noise | Effect |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **Precinct radio** | 1/4 | Restricted | Silent | Contact with dispatch and other Marshals. **Dead at Noise Clock 4+** (§2), which is exactly when it was needed. |
| **Sanction papers** | — | Restricted | Silent | The licence. Legal exemption from manslaughter law inside a designated containment zone. Not carrying them when asked is the offence, not lacking a Domain. |
| **Ledger balance book** | 1/4 | Common | Silent | Your debt, itemised, in someone else's handwriting. Not carried by choice. |
| **Bridge-tender's key** | 1/4 | Restricted | Quiet | Raises or lowers one bascule bridge in the Flats. Whoever holds one decides which side of an incursion the civilians are on. |
| **Beast-blood tattoo rig** | 1/2 | Common | Quiet | Cult equipment. Marks a member's rank; using one on a non-member is a recruitment, and both of you know it. |
