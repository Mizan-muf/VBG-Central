# Port Vane — Domains

**Status:** working draft · **Revised:** 2026-09-24
**Owns:** the Domain catalog, Stigma draw, Dead Static, the Sanction, and setting gear.

> Mechanics come from [VBG 2.2](../vbg-2.2/README.md). This document supplies a catalog, two DM-side scene rules, and gear entries. It adds **nothing** to what a Domain costs.

## 1. What a Domain is here

A Domain is an **Ability Domain** in VBG's sense — a DM-approved sphere of effect, produced by spending Strain, at 0 / 1 / 2 Strain for Minor / Standard / Major scale ([Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §5). Everything the rulebook says about scale, action cost, reach, sustaining, and hazards applies unchanged.

> **Strain is the whole cost.** Using a Domain in Port Vane costs exactly what it costs in base VBG and nothing more. There is no burnout track, no fatigue meter, no debt, no addiction counter, and no noise penalty attached to using an ability. A character with 4 Strain has four points of ability use in them, and spending them is the game working as intended.
>
> This is deliberate. Strain is already a hard limit — four points, spent one and two at a time, in a game where a Major Action is the only one you get. Stacking a second cost on top would make the interesting abilities the ones nobody presses.

What Port Vane adds is not a price. It is **what a Domain means to the people around you**:

- **It is licensed.** The Iron Ledger grants the Sanction, and the Sanction has to be kept current. See §4.
- **It is visible.** Domain use is unmistakable when it happens. Witnesses matter, and in this city most rooms have some.
- **Some things hunt it.** A few Carrion orient on Domain use above everything else. That is written on their cards, not on yours — see §2.

None of those three costs you a resource. All three produce scenes.

### Choosing one

Pick an entry from §3, or write your own to the same shape and have the DM approve it against [GM Guide](../vbg-2.2/GM%20Guide.md) §3. Every Domain here states three things, and yours should too:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Scope** | One sentence naming the medium. What the DM approved, and what arguments are settled against. |
| **Limit** | The concrete thing it cannot do. This is the field that makes a Domain a Domain rather than a wish, and it is what the approval standard asks for out loud. |
| **Signature cost** | The Success-with-Cost this Domain tends to pay. A suggestion, not a rule — the player still proposes the cost ([Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §2). |

**Nothing in this catalog deals more than 1 damage,** because nothing in VBG does. Domains are not distinguished by power. They are distinguished by what they reach, what they open, and what they cannot touch.

A character may hold up to three Domains, bought with PP at 20 each.

## 2. Stigma, Dead Static, and what hunts you

### Stigma draw

Carrion above Tier I are biologically addicted to Stigma: active Domain use reads to them as a flare on an empty horizon, and consuming a Domain user briefly grants fragments of that power ([Carrion](Carrion.md) §1).

**This lives on the creature card, never on the character sheet.** It is written into the **Senses** field and the **Stigma draw** field the [Creature Blueprint](../vbg-2.2/Creature%20Blueprint.md) §3 already requires, which makes it a targeting rule the DM runs:

> A creature with Stigma draw abandons whatever it was doing and comes for the last character who used a Domain in its reach.

Three consequences worth being clear about.

**It costs the player nothing.** No Strain, no roll, no clock, no mark on a sheet. The monster changes target. That is the entire mechanic.

**It is usable on purpose.** The most reliable way to pull a Carapace-Render off a crowd of civilians, or off a Marshal with a shotgun and no Domain, is to light something up where it can see you. Domain users in Port Vane are not liabilities to their crew; they are the ones who can *choose* to be the target. A Cleaver who understands this is worth three who do not.

**It is announced, like every threat.** The DM states the creature's Stigma draw in the scene post alongside its awareness, the same as any other card property. Nobody is ambushed by a rule.

**Not everything has it.** Shiverers hunt by smell and ground vibration and could not care less. Cairn-Titans detect everything in their footprint regardless. Stigma draw is a Tier II signature, and knowing which of the three is in front of you is most of the tactical problem.

### Dead Static

Dead Static is the electromagnetic deadening that surrounds Carrion activity: radios, phones, dashcams, electric light, and anything else needing current stop working.

**The DM declares it as a scene condition**, the same way they declare light or cover. It is not driven by anything the players spend or do. It applies:

- Within a **Cairn-Titan's** field, for as long as it is present ([Carrion](Carrion.md) §2).
- In a **static corridor**, permanently ([Setting Guide](Setting%20Guide.md) §2).
- For **one to two hours before a Rupture opens**, which is the city's only reliable early warning ([Setting Guide](Setting%20Guide.md) §3).

It bites the factions unevenly, which is most of its value:

- **Marshals** lose dispatch, which is how coordinated evacuation fails.
- **Contract crews** lose the bounty ticker and cannot call for a second crew.
- **Salvage** arrives at a cordon they cannot see into.
- **A character with Static Ear or Carry** becomes the only communication the group has — a reason to bring one, and a reason someone would take one from you.

Domain use does **not** cause Dead Static, is not suppressed by it, and has no interaction with it beyond the two Domains above.

## 3. The catalog

Twenty-four Domains, four in each of six niches. Written for this city: every one has a reason to exist in a place where the corpses are a commodity and the phones do not work.

### Force

Direct and destructive. Good at opening things and bad at everything requiring the room to stay intact.

| Domain | Scope | Limit | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kinetic** | Shoves, crushes, and redirects the momentum of things I can see. | Only masses a strong person could shift given leverage. It moves objects; people are moved by what hits them. | Something behind the target breaks too, and it belonged to someone. |
| **Flare** | Kindles and shapes open fire, whether it already burns or I start it by hand. | Fire needs fuel and air. Nothing in the rain, underwater, or in a sealed space without help. | The fire outlives the ability and goes somewhere you did not choose. |
| **Arc** | Draws current from a live or stored source and throws it. | No source, no ability. A dead grid is a dead Domain. | The source is spent — the lights, the truck battery, the thing you were going to need. |
| **Sunder** | Weakens the structural integrity of worked material I touch. | Worked material only, by touch. Not stone, not flesh, not anything living. | The weakening spreads: the floor, the stair, the wall you are standing against. |

### Control

The ground, the doors, and what is in the way. The workhorses of an incursion.

| Domain | Scope | Limit | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ballast** | Makes objects within sight heavier or lighter than they are. | Weight only. You never move the thing; you change what moving it costs. | It reverts at the worst moment, and whatever you lightened comes down. |
| **Tide** | Moves standing and pooled water, and whatever floats in it. | Standing water that is already there. Not rain, not the estuary, not what is inside a body. | What the water carries goes where the water goes, including the contamination. |
| **Bind** | Makes cable, rope, chain, and wire move and grip on command. | Cordage that is already present. You do not create it, and you cannot make it stronger than it is. | The line takes hold of the wrong thing, and letting go takes a cycle. |
| **Threshold** | Seals or unseals a doorway, gate, or gap for as long as I hold it. | An existing opening with a frame. Not open ground, and not a hole you just made. | It seals both ways, and somebody is on the wrong side. |

### Perception

Quiet, informational, and the reason a crew comes home. In a city with Dead Static, knowing things is a supply problem.

| Domain | Scope | Limit | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Residue** | Reads what recently happened in a place by touching its surfaces. | Hours, not days, and only where the surface was actually touched. | You get the moment, not the context, and it is somebody's worst one. |
| **Tell** | Reads physiological stress in a person I can see: heart, breath, sweat, tremor. | Bodies, not minds. It reads that they are lying; it never reads about what. | They notice you looking, and they are right about what it means. |
| **Bloom** | Extends awareness through an enclosed space as a felt map of what is in it. | Enclosed spaces. Outdoors it dissipates before it reaches anything. | You feel everything in the space, including what is inside the walls. |
| **Static Ear** | Hears through Dead Static and pulls signal out of interference. | You hear. You cannot transmit, and you cannot make anyone else hear it. | You hear something on the band that nobody transmitted. |

### Body

Yours, or someone else's. Three of these are what a crew's medic actually is.

| Domain | Scope | Limit | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Knit** | Closes wounds on a person I touch. *(A Strain Transfer ability — [Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §6 owns the procedure.)* | It moves Strain. It does not remove an Injury, and it never exceeds the transfer limit. | It closes wrong, and the Injury is now yours to explain. |
| **Anchor** | Makes my own body immovable where it stands. | Your own body, and only while you stay still. Immovable is not invulnerable. | Immovable includes not being able to leave. |
| **Stride** | Moves my own body faster and further than a body should go. | Your own body, over ground you can see, in something close to a straight line. | You arrive before your footing does. |
| **Hollow** | Takes harm meant for someone within reach onto myself instead. | Declared before the harm lands, on someone within reach, one threat at a time. | You take it, and you take it whole — including the Injury. |

### Voice

Sound, speech, and crowds. Underrated until the radios die.

| Domain | Scope | Limit | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hush** | Dampens sound in a space I can see. | It dampens the whole space. You cannot silence one person and leave the rest. | No warning shouts, no listening for what is coming, for anyone. |
| **Carry** | Sends my voice to one person I have met, anywhere in the district. | One person at a time, someone you have actually met, inside the district. | It carries both ways for a moment, and they hear where you are. |
| **Press** | Puts unbearable weight behind a single spoken command. | One short command, obeyed literally, once. It cannot be a sentence and cannot be repeated on the same person. | It works, and they remember exactly what was done to them. |
| **Chorus** | Makes a crowd feel what I feel. | A crowd. On a single listener it does nothing at all. | A crowd is a poor instrument. They keep feeling it after you stop. |

### Anti-Carrion

Domains that do something specific to the things that come through tears. Rare, valuable, and the reason Salvage will hire someone with no other qualification.

| Domain | Scope | Limit | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rot** | Accelerates decay in dead organic matter I touch. | Dead matter, by touch. It does nothing whatsoever to anything alive. | The corpse is worth less. Salvage noticed. |
| **Solvent** | Dissolves the bonds holding grafted matter together. | Grafted and bonded matter. Not original tissue, and not solid manufactured material. | It does not distinguish grafted from original, on anyone. |
| **Rust** | Corrodes metal, including the inorganic grafts in Carrion plate. | Metal, and all of it in reach — your weapon, the catwalk, the cuffs, the railing you are holding. | Everything metal nearby corrodes, at the worst possible moment. |
| **Quarantine** | Holds a Rupture, or a wound in a Carrion, shut for as long as I hold it. | One opening, sustained. The moment you stop, it opens. | Holding it is a sustained effect, and letting go is worse than never starting. |

**How the anti-Carrion four actually work.** They do not add damage. They satisfy **creature conditions** ([Creature Blueprint](../vbg-2.2/Creature%20Blueprint.md) §3.3): Solvent and Rust crack a Carapace-Render's plate so ordinary attacks can remove boxes at all, Rot ends a Shiverer's cohesion without a fight and ruins the salvage, and Quarantine is how a Cairn stops opening secondary tears while the demolition crew works. That is why a Salvage Factor will pay for a Rust user who cannot fight: the whole engagement changes length.

## 4. The Sanction

The Sanction is the licence. It is legal exemption from manslaughter law for Domain use inside a designated containment zone, and it is the Iron Ledger's actual product — not the medicine, not the weapons. **Permission.**

> **You do not owe the Ledger money. You owe it work.**

A Sanction is granted, and then it has to be kept current. Keeping it current means taking contracts, showing up to cordons, and being a name on a crew list. A Cleaver who stops working does not accumulate a balance; they stop being licensed, and in Port Vane an unlicensed Domain user is not a private citizen. They are a listing.

**This is the engine the campaign runs on**, and it needs no track, no number, and no bookkeeping. It runs entirely on the DM's authority over what the world does back ([Campaign Format](Campaign%20Format.md) §2).

| Standing | What it means in play |
|---|---|
| **Current** | You can use a Domain in a containment zone and walk away from it. Doors open. You are expected at the next cordon. |
| **Lapsing** | You have not worked. Nobody has said anything yet, and that is itself the message. |
| **Revoked** | You are a Stray with a file. Every previous use is now retroactively a charge somebody could bring. |
| **Never held** | Most Strays. Every public Domain use is evidence, so you learn to use it where nobody is looking. |

**Why this is better than a debt.** A balance is a number that goes down when you work and up when you rest, and it turns every session into arithmetic. A licence is a relationship with an institution that wants something from you. It produces the same grip — you cannot simply walk away from the Ledger — and it produces scenes instead of subtraction.

**The other three positions.** The Marshals hold no Domain licence at all, so an officer with a Domain is unlicensed by definition — a Stray in uniform, and a career's worth of blackmail for anyone who works it out. The Cult refuses the Sanction as the thing itself: not the drug, not the price, the *permission*. And a Stray simply never signed, which costs them every room the licence opens and buys them the only position in the city with nothing to revoke.

## 5. Strain, stabilisers, and the corpse

This is the one place Port Vane **overrides** a VBG rule rather than adding to it, and the override is stated here in full.

> **Strain does not come back on its own.** In Port Vane, **Catch Your Breath and Full Rest restore no Strain**. Rest heals bruises and clears your head; it does not refill the thing a Domain runs on.
>
> Strain returns from exactly two sources, and both of them are a dead Carrion.

| Source | Rule |
|---|---|
| **A stabiliser dose** | Refined Carrion tissue, bought or issued. See the table below. |
| **Raw Carrion tissue** | Eaten off a fresh corpse. See below. |

**Strain Transfer is untouched** ([Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §6). It moves Strain between people at 1:1 and creates none, so a medic with Knit still works exactly as written — they are redistributing a scarce resource, not making it. In a city where Strain is scarce, that makes them considerably more important than they were.

**What this does to the campaign.** You fight Carrion to get the thing that lets you fight Carrion. The Iron Ledger's Salvage division refines every dose in the city, which is the monopoly stated as a mechanic rather than as a paragraph of fiction. No balance, no interest, no arithmetic — just the fact that the only thing that refills you has to be cut out of something that was trying to kill you.

And it makes the Rupture Cult correct about one thing, which is the point of them: **you do not have to buy it refined.** It is right there on the floor.

### Consuming raw Carrion

**Major Action**, within Melee / Reach of a Carrion corpse killed in this scene, before it rots or dissolves.

- Restore **2 Strain**.
- **The corpse drops one bounty band** ([Carrion](Carrion.md) §4). A Tier I corpse becomes worth nothing at all.
- Once per corpse, per character.

It costs no Strain, no roll, and nothing on your sheet. What it costs is the reason everyone came, and whatever the people watching you do about it afterwards. A Cleaver who does this in front of their crew has ended a career. A Stray who does it in an alley has solved the evening.

This is also, precisely, how the Hollowed start. The Cult does not need to lie to recruit: they offer a supply line that is free, immediate, and entirely real, and they are honest that it changes you. Whether it changes you is a fiction question the DM answers slowly, not a track ([Factions](Factions.md) §5).

## 6. Setting gear

Written to the [Item Glossary](../vbg-2.2/Item%20Glossary.md) format and bands. Everything in the base glossary is available in Port Vane; these are the entries the setting adds.

### Stabilisers

Refined Carrion tissue. The most valuable product of the corpse trade, and the only manufactured thing in the city that puts Strain back.

| Item | Slot | Band | Noise | Effect | Uses |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **Stabiliser** | 1/4 | Restricted | Silent | Minor Action: restore **2 Strain** to yourself or someone within reach. | Single use |
| **Red stabiliser** | 1/4 | **Rare** | Silent | Hastens regeneration. **Restores no Strain on its own.** Taken together with a stabiliser in the same action, that dose restores **all** Strain instead of 2. Taken in downtime, removes **one Injury**, including a Lasting one. | Single use |

**The one-consumable-per-scene limit in [Item Glossary](../vbg-2.2/Item%20Glossary.md) §4 does not apply to stabilisers.** It exists to stop healing items stacking in a game where Strain comes back on its own. Here it does not, so the limit would simply mean a character runs out and stays out. A character may use as many doses as they have — and the constraint that matters is that they are Restricted, refined by one organisation, and carried at 1/4 slot each.

A red stabiliser is worth a Career bounty on its own. Nobody sells one; they are issued, recovered, or owed.

### Salvage and munitions

| Item | Slot | Band | Noise | Effect | Uses |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **Salvage kit** | 1 | Restricted | Silent | Required to recover a Carrion corpse's value. **Without one, a bounty pays one band lower**, and a Tier I corpse pays nothing at all. Contents: bone saw, gland flasks, cut-resistant gauntlets, cold pack. | 4 corpses |
| **Anti-anomaly munitions** | 1/4 | Restricted | **Loud** | Refined from Carrion tissue. Counts as a **breaching tool** against a Carrion creature condition at any band a firearm reaches — the standard answer to a Carapace-Render's plate. Requires a firearm. | Stack of 6 |
| **Scent-mask, diesel and sulfur** | 1/4 | Common | Silent | Carrion that hunt by smell — Shiverers above all — treat the wearer as **one awareness step lower**. Does nothing against sight, sound, or Stigma draw. | One scene |
| **Dampening cuffs** | 1/2 | **Rare** | Silent | A restrained person cannot use a Domain. Ledger Cellar equipment; possessing a pair outside the Ledger is itself a charge. | — |

### City equipment

| Item | Slot | Band | Noise | Effect |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **Sanction papers** | — | Restricted | Silent | The licence (§4). Not carrying them when asked is the offence, not having a Domain. |
| **Precinct radio** | 1/4 | Restricted | Silent | Contact with dispatch and other Marshals. **Dead under Dead Static** (§2), which is exactly when it was needed. |
| **Bridge-tender's key** | 1/4 | Restricted | Quiet | Raises or lowers one bascule bridge in the Flats. Whoever holds one decides which side of an incursion the civilians are on. |
| **Crew list** | 1/4 | Common | Silent | Who is on a contract, in the dispatcher's handwriting. Being on one is how a Sanction stays current; being taken off one is how a career ends without anyone saying so. |
