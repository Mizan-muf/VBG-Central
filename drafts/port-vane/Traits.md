# Port Vane — Traits

**Status:** working draft · **Revised:** 2026-09-24
**Owns:** the Major and Minor Trait catalog for this setting.

> Mechanics come from [VBG 2.2](../vbg-2.2/README.md). This is a catalog of examples, not a closed list and not a class system.

## 1. How to use this

A character takes **one Major Trait (+2)** and **one Minor Trait (+1)** at creation, and buys more with PP ([Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §3, §8). At most one relevant Major and one relevant Minor apply to any single roll, however many the character owns.

Three things worth saying before the tables.

**Nothing here is a class.** A Bone-Saw can hold a badge, a Patrol Officer can be Tethered, and a Green-Mark can be the most dangerous person in the room. Traits describe what someone has actually done, not what they are allowed to do. Membership is a separate thing entirely and lives in [Campaign Format](Campaign%20Format.md) §3.

**A Major Trait is not a faction rank.** Taking **Line Cleaver** does not make you a Chartered Contractor in good standing, and taking **Patrol Officer** does not get you a precinct. The trait is the competence. The rank is fiction the DM tracks ([Factions](Factions.md) §7).

**The player asserts relevance.** VBG has the player claim which Traits apply in the post, and the DM rejects the claim only when the fiction does not support it ([Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §2). The *Does not cover* column exists to make those rejections predictable rather than personal — it is guidance, not an exhaustive prohibition.

## 2. Major Traits (+2)

### Iron Ledger — Contract

| Trait | Who you are | Covers | Does not cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Line Cleaver** | A licensed crew operator who has fought inside a cordon and come back. | Carrion combat, crew tactics, cordon procedure, reading a bounty listing. | Anything outside a containment zone's rules, where your licence is just paper. |
| **Ringmaster** | A Director who runs crews, cameras, and the bid. | Command, staging a kill for witnesses, bidding, moving corporate money. | Doing the work yourself. You have not held a weapon under pressure in years. |
| **Breach Specialist** | You put holes in buildings for a living, legally. | Demolition, structural weak points, ordnance, opening a Cairn's shell. | Subtlety of any kind. Everything you know how to do is Loud. |
| **Bonded Killer** | You bought the Sanction and you have used it. | Intimidation, killing efficiently, knowing exactly what the exemption covers. | Being trusted, by anyone, including your own crew. |

### Iron Ledger — Salvage

| Trait | Who you are | Covers | Does not cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bone-Saw** | A field butcher working a cordon before the corpse dissolves. | Harvesting, Carrion anatomy, working fast and precisely beside something still twitching. | Fighting anything healthy. You arrive after. |
| **Apothecary** | You refine tissue into stabilisers, and you know what is in them. | Chemistry, dosing, spotting adulterated product, and knowing which batch of stabilisers is worth what someone is asking. | Anything above street-lab scale. You do not run the firm. |
| **Carrion Broker** | You sell the parts and you know the floor. | Appraisal, negotiating value, knowing who wants what and what they will not admit to wanting. | Honesty. People who deal with you twice have learned something. |

### District Marshals

| Trait | Who you are | Covers | Does not cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Patrol Officer** | You walk a beat in a paranormal city with a shotgun and no Domain licence. | Procedure, the district's people, holding a line, buckshot and barricades. | Authority over Ledger contractors, who will tell you so. |
| **Coroner-Cop** | A detective who reads bodies and case files. | Investigation, evidence, establishing what killed this and when. | Making anyone act on what you found. |
| **Evacuation Lead** | The person who gets civilians out, which is the only thing the Marshals exist to do. | Crowd movement, routes, triage under fire, talking frightened people into moving. | Winning a fight. You were never supposed to be in one. |
| **Desk-Warden** | You run a precinct on a budget that was redirected to a defence contract. | Logistics, paperwork as a weapon, knowing where the money went and who signed. | Field work. Your knees and your reputation are both office-grade. |

### Strays

| Trait | Who you are | Covers | Does not cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Holdout Warden** | You protect specific blocks, and everyone on them knows your face. | That block's people, routes, and rooftops; barricades; standing between something and a door. | Anywhere else in the city, where you are nobody with a reputation for trouble. |

|---|---|---|---|
| **Dock Rat** | Raised on the waterfront, the Black Ditch, and the tidal flats. | Boats, water, the Sinks, container stacks, what the tide does to a body. | The Spire, where you are visibly from somewhere else. |
| **Wire Hand** | An electrician and phone-line tech in a city that keeps losing both. | Current, comms, jury-rigging, working around Dead Static, knowing which lines are still live. | Anything that needs the grid to be up. |
| **Bridge-Tender** | You run a bascule bridge in the Flats, or you used to. | The mechanism, the chokepoints, the schedule, and who bribed whom to raise it. | Explaining the last time you raised it. |
| **Field Medic** | Trauma medicine, mostly without a licence. | Stabilisation, Injuries, keeping someone alive long enough to reach a truck. | A Domain. You do this with your hands and a kit. |
| **Wheelman** | You drive anything, including things with a failing transmission. | Vehicles, routes, Lead-Sled convoys, the Bleed-Belt ([Setting Guide](Setting%20Guide.md) §5). | Anything on foot, which you consider a planning failure. |

### Marked

These are things that happened to you. Each is genuinely useful somewhere, which is the point.

| Trait | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Ash Taste** | You can tell refined tissue from cut tissue, and a good dose from a bad one, by taste alone. |
| **Scar Tissue** | Enduring pain, and being visibly someone who has already survived worse than this. |
| **Static Deaf** | Long exposure to Dead Static. You read a room by vibration and air pressure instead of sound. |
| **Marked Face** | You are recognised on sight in certain districts. Useful exactly when being recognised helps. |
| **Scrounged** | You have kept yourself supplied without a licence for years. You know every unofficial source of a dose in your ring. |
| **Cold Read** | You have been lied to professionally enough to spot the shape of it. |

| **Lapsed** | Your Sanction is gone and everyone knows. It shuts every official door and opens the ones that only open for people with nothing to protect. |

## 4. Writing your own

The catalogs are examples ([Player Rulebook](../vbg-2.2/Player%20Rulebook.md) §3). A trait you write yourself is fine and often better, provided it passes the same two tests the entries above do:

1. **It names something you have actually done**, not something you are good at in general. *Wheelman* is a trait. *Skilled* is not.
2. **You can say when it does not apply.** A Major Trait that helps on every roll is not a Major Trait; it is a Stat, and you already have three.

A DM approving a trait asks for the second one out loud. If neither of you can name a situation the trait sits out, narrow it until you can.
