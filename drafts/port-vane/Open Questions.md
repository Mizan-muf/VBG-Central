# Port Vane — Open Questions

**Status:** working draft · **Revised:** 2026-09-24
**Owns:** unresolved setting and module questions.

> A pending entry is not a rule. Nothing here is playable until settled.

Setting questions carry **P** numbers. Rules questions belong to the [VBG 2.2 Decision Log](../vbg-2.2/Decision%20Log.md) and are referenced, not duplicated.

## 1. Open

| ID | Question | What a ruling must state |
|---|---|---|
| **P2** | **How is faction standing tracked?** | One track per faction or a single reputation; what moves it; whether gaining with one automatically costs another, and if so for which pairs. Until settled, standing is narrated fiction, not a number. P3 is now settled without it, so this blocks nothing. |
| **P10** | **What does running out actually look like?** Strain recovers only from stabilisers or raw tissue ([Domains](Domains.md) §5), so a character can reach a state of having a Domain and no way to use it. Nothing says how long that lasts or how a group gets out of it. | Whether a dry character has any route back that does not require a corpse — a Ledger advance, a Warden's charity, a Cult offer — or whether finding one is simply the next scene. Not blocking; a DM can rule it at the table. |
| **P8** | **What does a faction clock look like on paper?** | [Campaign Format](Campaign%20Format.md) §6 requires each faction to run two or three clocks that advance on schedule. A format — segments, visibility to players, who advances them, what a DM does when two clocks collide — would make that practice consistent across DMs. Not blocking; the principle works without a template. |

## 2. Blocked on VBG 2.2

**Nothing is currently blocked.** Every VBG item that gated this set was settled on 2026-09-24 and carried into the documents:

| VBG item | Resolution | What it unblocked here |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** — Downed, injury, death | Downed inflicts an Injury and starts a deadline; death requires a stated killing or an announced lethal danger. | Carrion cards can now say which creatures kill. Only Tier III does by default. |
| **D2** — items and economy | Full Item Glossary, with four acquisition paths and Common / Restricted / Rare value bands. | Stabilisers, salvage, and bounty bands ([Carrion](Carrion.md) §4). |
| **Q5** — damage | Every hit deals exactly 1, permanently. Weapons grant effects, not damage. | Carrion tiers are now written as conditions and defeat requirements rather than box counts. |
| **Q2** — answering threats | No defensive roll. Address with the Major Action, answer with a conditional, or take it. | Incursion combat runs. |

See the [VBG Decision Log](../vbg-2.2/Decision%20Log.md) §2a–§2c. As of the 2026-09-24 carry-through pass, **nothing in VBG 2.2 is pending at all**: D3 (PP awards) and D5a (Transition Scene) are both settled, which makes progression and the [Bleed-Belt](Setting%20Guide.md) §5 runnable.

One correction worth recording here, because this module relied on it. Between 2026-09-23 and the carry-through pass, the VBG Decision Log listed rulings that had never been written into the Player Rulebook or the Item Glossary — including the Downed-and-death procedure this set cites on every Carrion card. A Port Vane DM reading the rulebook in that window would have found the opposite rule. The lesson for this document set: **cite the rule, then check the rule is there.**

## 3. Settled

Recorded so they are not reopened by accident.

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| 2026-09-23 | Player-centric campaign. Not West Marches; no expedition loop or fixed quest window. |
| 2026-09-23 | Factions hold power. Players work inside, between, and against them rather than displacing them. |
| 2026-09-23 | Strays are a status, not a faction. Every character starts unaffiliated. |
| 2026-09-23 | Three factions: the Iron Ledger (Contract and Salvage divisions), the District Marshals, the Rupture Cult. |
| 2026-09-23 | Players decide what their characters do; only a DM decides what the world does back. Self-run scenes are logged and ratified in batch. |
| 2026-09-23 | Marshals are the people's faction — the only one profiting nothing from an incursion, and the only one open to Domainless characters. |
| **2026-09-24** | **No faction is an antagonist.** All four bodies are playable, each with a coherent aim and a sympathetic reading. The **Carrion** are the campaign's threat, and the only one. *Supersedes the 2026-09-23 entry naming the Rupture Cult as primary antagonist.* ([Factions](Factions.md) §1) |
| **2026-09-24** | **P1 settled.** An original 24-entry Port Vane Domain catalog replaces the external generic list, spread across force, control, perception, body, voice, and anti-Carrion niches. Each entry states **Scope**, **Limit**, and a **signature cost**. ([Domains](Domains.md) §3) |
| **2026-09-24** | **P3 settled.** Opposed demands from two memberships force a choice: comply openly, comply concealed, or refuse both. No roll. The DM plays out what each body does about it. ([Campaign Format](Campaign%20Format.md) §3) |
| **2026-09-24** | **P5 settled.** Dollar figures stay as fiction. Bounties pay in four bands — Petty, Standing, Substantial, Career — mapped onto the VBG Item Glossary value bands. Bands do not make change, and salvage recovery, not the kill, is what pays. Anything eaten for Strain drops the corpse one band. ([Carrion](Carrion.md) §4) |
| **2026-09-24** | **P6 settled.** Tier IV is retained as a slow background presence, not a metaplot. Nothing is gated behind discovering them, no clock counts down to a reveal, and a campaign that never touches them has lost nothing. ([Carrion](Carrion.md) §2.4) |
| **2026-09-24** | **P7 settled.** The Rupture Cult is fully playable and no membership pairing is forbidden. Cross-faction pairs are Concealed or Dangerous rather than banned, which gives six workable combinations plus the Stray path. Internal Ledger politics remains one source of divided loyalty among several. ([Campaign Format](Campaign%20Format.md) §3) |
| **2026-09-24** | **Remaining a Stray is a complete campaign path**, not a waiting room: no obligations and access to every room, traded against no supply line. ([Factions](Factions.md) §2) |
| **2026-09-24** | **Stabilisers, salvage kits, anti-anomaly munitions, dampening cuffs, and city equipment have entries** using the VBG Item Glossary's format and bands. ([Domains](Domains.md) §6) |
| **2026-09-24** | **The Domain catalog is written.** [Domains](Domains.md) exists as a document. *Until this date the file was cited by six documents and did not exist.* |
| **2026-09-24** | **An original Port Vane trait catalog replaces the v1.4.2 lists.** [Traits](Traits.md) holds 24 Major and 32 Minor Traits grouped by faction, trade, experience, and connection, each with a *does not cover* column so that relevance disputes resolve predictably. VBG 2.2 ships no catalog of its own (its Q14), so this is the only one a Port Vane table needs. |
| **2026-09-24** | **P4 settled.** Ranks are **fictional standing only**. They open doors, assign obligations, and change how people speak to a character; they grant no bonus and cost no PP. This keeps rank inside the DM's authority over what the world does back, rather than turning it into a purchase. ([Factions](Factions.md) §7) |
| **2026-09-24, second pass** | **The debt economy is cut, and so is Burnout.** No balances, no interest, no Ledger ledger, no 0–4 track, no Overreach. *Supersedes the Burnout and debt-pipeline entries above.* Strain is the only thing using a Domain costs. The Ledger's grip is now the **Sanction** — a licence kept current by working — plus its monopoly on refining. ([Domains](Domains.md) §4, [Factions](Factions.md) §3) |
| **2026-09-24, second pass** | **Domain loudness is cut.** Domains carry no noise rating and do not tick the Noise Clock. *Supersedes the 2026-09-23 loudness entry and its 2026-09-24 successor.* What Carrion react to is now **Stigma draw**, a field on the creature card: a creature that has it abandons its target for the last Domain user in reach. It costs the player nothing and can be baited on purpose. Domains are distinguished by a **Limit** column instead. ([Domains](Domains.md) §2) |
| **2026-09-24, second pass** | **Dead Static is decoupled from the clock.** It is a scene condition the DM declares, like light or cover, in three standing cases: a Cairn-Titan's field, a static corridor, and the hours before a Rupture. ([Domains](Domains.md) §2) |
| **2026-09-24, second pass** | **Strain does not recover on its own here.** Catch Your Breath and Full Rest restore no Strain in Port Vane. Only a **stabiliser** (2 Strain) or **raw Carrion tissue** (Major Action, 2 Strain, drops the corpse one bounty band) puts it back. Strain Transfer is untouched. **This is the module's only override of a VBG rule**, and it replaces debt as the campaign engine: you hunt Carrion to get the thing that lets you hunt Carrion. ([Domains](Domains.md) §5) |
| **2026-09-24, second pass** | **Red stabilisers are the rare grade.** They restore nothing alone; taken with a stabiliser, that dose restores all Strain, and taken in downtime they remove an Injury. Grey stabilisers are cut entirely. ([Domains](Domains.md) §6) |
| **2026-09-24** | **The world runs without players.** Faction clocks are written before session one and advance on schedule rather than on player attention. A DM should be able to state what would have happened had nobody posted. ([Campaign Format](Campaign%20Format.md) §6) |
